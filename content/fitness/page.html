Vantix is the flagship. Its free core is in progress; until it ships, five free calculators and a field guide cover the ground: {{n:total}} entries on hypertrophy, powerlifting, meet day, nutrition, diet and health. Everything runs in your browser.

I’m not a coach or a dietitian. These are working notes from someone who trains. Each one names its source and says how well supported it is. They’re general information, not medical advice.

“Effective reps” is a coaching shorthand: the idea that the reps closest to failure do most of the work. Drag the sliders to see how a set scores under it, then tally a session.

    Read this as a heuristic, not a measurement. No study has tested a cut-off at five reps. What the research supports is softer: growth tends to improve as sets end nearer to failure, and going all the way to failure adds little over stopping just short. {{c:nuckols,robinson2024,refalo2023}}
