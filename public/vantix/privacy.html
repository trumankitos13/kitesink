01

Our stance

Vantix is a periodization OS: it only works if you connect the apps, wearables, logs and labs that describe your whole training and health life. We treat that as a serious responsibility, not a growth lever.

The short version: we collect what we need to run the product for you, we keep it transparent and portable, and we never monetize it beyond the subscription you pay.

02

What we store

Everything you import or connect lands on one universal event spine. We keep two layers, deliberately:

RAW PAYLOAD
The original export or API response, stored verbatim and unchanged — so any derived value can be re-computed and audited.
NORMALIZED EVENT
The canonical event we derive from it (hrv, set, macro, sleep, lab) used to power the surfaces.
ACCOUNT
Email, authentication and subscription status. That’s the extent of the identity we hold.
03

What we never do

Some lines we will not cross, by design:

NO SELLING
We never sell, rent or share your data with advertisers, insurers or brokers.
NO SILENT AI
We never feed your data to a model you didn’t explicitly opt into.
NO LOCK-IN
We never withhold your data to keep you subscribed — export is always available.
04

Integrations & sources

You choose what to connect. Live connections (via our aggregator) and manual file imports both flow through the same import engine and the same privacy rules.

We request the narrowest scope each source allows, and a missed or revoked connection is shown to you rather than quietly back-filled. Disconnect a source any time; its already-imported events stay yours to keep or delete.

05

AI & the co-pilot

The AI co-pilot reads the parts of your plan and history needed to propose a change — and it can only ever propose. Every suggestion is a diff you review; nothing is written without your confirmation through the review-gate.

06

Your controls

You hold the switches:

EXPORT
Download your raw payloads and normalized events at any time, in open formats.
DELETE
Delete individual sources, a date range, or your entire account — hard deletes, not flags.
OPT-IN
Every AI and data-sharing feature is off until you turn it on.
07

Security & retention

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is scoped to the systems that run the product, and we keep your data only while your account is active or as long as the law requires.

When you delete data, it is removed from primary storage immediately and purged from backups on their normal rotation. We’ll tell you the current window on request.

08

Changes & contact

If this summary changes materially, we’ll surface it in-app before it takes effect — not bury it in a diff. Substantive history is kept so you can see what changed and when.

Privacy questions, export requests or deletion requests: privacy@vantix.app. We aim to respond within a few business days.
