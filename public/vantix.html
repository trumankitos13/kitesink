§ 01 — The core

Three things, done better than anything else. Everything else, integrated.

These pillars are the product’s core, and they’re free forever. We charge for the integrations and the intelligence around them — never for the programming brain itself.

Programmable periodization

You define the structure — macrocycle, mesocycle, microcycle, session, group, exercise, set — and the engine enforces it. Volume landmarks, effective-set math and phase caps are computed continuously, not eyeballed.

  • MV / MEV / MAV / MRV landmarks per group
  • Effective volume = direct + Σ(synergist × rate)
  • Phase MRV caps · concurrent-cardio interference

Integration & normalization

Connect 500+ apps or drop any export. Everything is translated into one unified format on a universal event spine, deduplicated and coverage-tracked — so a missed export is visible and nothing maps silently.

  • Any source → one universal event spine
  • Raw payloads kept verbatim, re-derivable
  • Human-confirmed resolution queue

AI co-pilot with a review-gate

Chat to build or adjust a plan. The co-pilot never writes directly — it produces a reviewable diff that passes the same hard-rule validators as your manual edits. You accept, reject or edit each change.

  • Chat → proposed diff → validators → confirm
  • Row-level accept / reject / edit
  • Blocked on any failed rule — no overrides

§ 03 — How it works

From a folder of exports to a validated, living plan.

Five stages. Each is inspectable — nothing happens to your program that you can’t see and didn’t approve.

  1. Step 01

    Connect

    Sync 500+ apps live, or drag in any export. File import is free forever and works for sources no aggregator supports.

  2. Step 02

    Normalize

    Every source is unified onto the event spine, deduped and matched to your library. Unknowns wait in a resolution queue for your confirmation.

  3. Step 03

    Program

    Define your mesocycle and macrocycle — set targets, landmarks, priorities — by hand or with the co-pilot.

  4. Step 04

    Review-gate

    Every change becomes a diff and runs the hard-rule validators. Failures block until resolved.

  5. Step 05

    Lock & sync

    Confirm the week, lock the baseline, and let live data drive auto-progression proposals next cycle.

§ 04 — The defining bet

The AI proposes. The rules decide. You confirm.

Most AI coaches write straight to your plan and hope. Vantix’s co-pilot can’t. Every change it suggests becomes a code-review-style diff. The same hard-rule validators that guard your manual edits run against it. If a rule fails, the change is blocked until you resolve it.

It can suggest anything. It can apply nothing you didn’t accept.

§ 06 — Scaling

Built for the advanced. Bridged to everyone else.

Vantix’s default is full depth — for the lifter who already thinks in MEV, MRV, RIR and TDEE. But the same engine bridges down: a difficulty level, set at onboarding and changeable anytime, decides how much surface you see. The math always runs underneath — difficulty only changes how much of it shows. You can always climb; nothing decides for you that you can’t handle it.

How much of Vantix shows at each difficulty level
LevelModeWhat you seeSurface vs engine
Beginner Guided Core lifts and simple volume targets in plain language. The landmark math runs underneath but stays hidden until you ask for it. Shown Running
Intermediate Bridged MEV / MRV bands, RIR logging and the weekly review appear — each with guardrails and a short explanation of why it moved. Shown Running
Advanceddefault Full instrument Effective-volume math, phase caps, the mesocycle planner, the co-pilot and all seven surfaces. Nothing withheld, nothing dumbed down. Shown Running
