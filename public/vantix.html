§ 03 — HOW IT WORKS

From a folder of exports to a validated, living plan.

Five stages. Each is inspectable — nothing happens to your program that you can't see and didn't approve.

STEP 01
Connect

Sync 500+ apps live, or drag in any export. File import is free forever and works for sources no aggregator supports.

STEP 02
Normalize

Every source is unified onto the event spine, deduped and matched to your library. Unknowns wait in a resolution queue for your confirmation.

STEP 03
Program

Define your mesocycle and macrocycle — set targets, landmarks, priorities — by hand or with the co-pilot.

STEP 04
Review-gate

Every change becomes a diff and runs the hard-rule validators. Failures block until resolved.

STEP 05
Lock & sync

Confirm the week, lock the baseline, and let live data drive auto-progression proposals next cycle.
