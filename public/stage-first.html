Get paid, safely

Held until the gig happens.

Send an offer, the money’s held — not spent — and released to the player after the show. If tonight falls through, cancel up to 24 hours before for a full refund. No chasing a payment at 1am.

  • Held before the show, released after the set
  • Cancel-friendly up to 24h before — full refund
  • Either side can report a problem, which freezes the release for review

Test mode today. The whole payment flow runs in test mode. No real money moves yet.

A booking’s states, in the thread

  1. OfferSent in the chat. Waiting for the player to accept or pass.
  2. AcceptedReady for the payment step.
  3. HeldThe player is locked in. The fee is held and releases after the gig.
  4. ReleasedOn its way to the player. The booker rates the gig.

A booking can also end as declined, cancelled or refunded, or sit frozen for review when someone reports a problem.

Closeout Design stage · not built

One report both sides can stand behind.

After the show, the ticket reports for the night go in. What comes out is one approved report that the promoter and the artist’s team can both stand behind. The promoter can approve it without an account.

Where this side stands. It is designed and specified, not built. A small pilot, with closeouts run by hand, is being planned.

Ask about a pilot stagefirst.app ↗

  1. 1

    Sources in

    The ticket reports for the show go in as they arrive, each one kept with the show it belongs to.

  2. 2

    Differences flagged

    Where two reports disagree, the difference is flagged for a person to review.

  3. 3

    A report version

    The reviewed numbers become one version of the report. A correction makes a new version.

  4. 4

    Approved, no account needed

    The promoter approves that exact version through a verified link, without signing up for anything.
