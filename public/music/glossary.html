34 terms, in plain words.

Every term the music field guide uses, defined once. Search, or narrow by area.

General information on how music rights and royalties work, not legal advice. For a contract, a termination notice or a dispute, talk to a music lawyer.

AI-assisted work and AI-generated output
SOCAN's split: an AI-assisted work is made by a person using AI as a tool; an AI-generated output is made with minimal human involvement and cannot be registered. AI and music rights · Canada, US
SOCAN 2025b
Artist, Performer and Sound Recording Owner (SoundExchange's terms)
SoundExchange uses Artist for the name on the release, Performer for each person who makes up that Artist, and Sound Recording Owner for whoever owns the master, which is separate from owning the song. Neighbouring rights · US
SoundExchange n.d.-c
Black box
Informal industry name for a pool of collected royalties that nobody has been able to allocate. It is a nickname, not a legal or official term; collectives use names such as suspense account. Unclaimed royalties · International
Blanket licence (performing right)
One licence that lets a user perform any work in a society's repertoire, including foreign works it represents under reciprocal deals, with no song-by-song clearance. Performance royalties · International
WIPO 2012
Blanket license (digital)
Since 1 January 2021, a qualifying digital service can get one license through the MLC that covers every song open to the compulsory license, in place of song-by-song notices. Mechanicals and the MLC · US as of 19 November 2026
17 USC 115 · Copyright Office: MMA FAQ
Compulsory (mechanical) license
A US legal route to record and distribute your own version of a song once recordings of it have been released in the US with the owner's permission. You must meet the conditions and pay the statutory rate. Mechanicals and the MLC · US
17 USC 115
Cue sheet
A list of the music in a TV or film programme, with each work and use, sent to a society so it can pay the writers and publishers. Performance royalties · International
Digital replica
In the NO FAKES bill (S. 4591, not law as of November 2026), a newly made, computer-generated, highly realistic copy of a person's voice or likeness. Authorised remixes, samples and remasters are excluded. AI and music rights · US as of 20 November 2026
GPO 2026
Featured and non-featured performers
A featured artist is the person or group most prominent on a track. Non-featured performers, such as session musicians and backing singers, are paid through a separate route. Neighbouring rights · US
SoundExchange n.d.-d · USCCLIIC n.d.-c
ISRC code
International Standard Recording Code: a 12-character identifier for one recording, made of a rightsholder code, a year and a five-digit number. Royalty basics · International, UK
PPL n.d.-b
ISWC code
International Standard Musical Work Code: a number that identifies a song and links it to its rights holders in the society systems. Royalty basics · International
WIPO 2012
Letter of direction (LOD)
Under 17 USC 114(g)(5), an instruction from a payee of the recording owner's or featured artists' share to the designated collective (SoundExchange) to pay part of it to the recording's producer, mixer or sound engineer. Producer royalties · US as of 20 November 2026
USCCLIIC n.d.-c · U.S. Copyright Office n.d.-a
Market-share distribution
How the statute says remaining unclaimed accrued royalties are paid: to identified owners in proportion to their relative market shares in the usage reports, with a share passed on to songwriters. Mechanicals and the MLC · US
17 USC 115
Mechanical right
The right to record a song and to make copies of that recording and distribute them. In the US, streaming and download services get it for songs through the MLC's blanket licence. Royalty basics · International, US
WIPO 2012 · TMLC n.d.-d
Musical work (the song)
The composition: the music plus any words that go with it. Its authors are the people who wrote the music and lyrics, unless it was made for hire. Royalty basics · US
U.S. Copyright Office n.d.-d
Neighbouring (related) rights
Rights that performers, record producers and broadcasters hold, separate from the copyright in the song. Also called related rights. Societies in many countries manage them for broadcasts and public play of recordings. Royalty basics · International
WIPO 2012
Notice of intention (NOI)
The notice a person serves on a song's copyright owner, or files with the Copyright Office when the owner cannot be found in its records, to use the compulsory license for physical copies. Mechanicals and the MLC · US
17 USC 115
Overclaim
A song where the shares claimed by all members together come to more than 100 percent. The MLC's Overclaims Tool lets owners amend or give up shares and upload documents while it is resolved. Mechanicals and the MLC · US as of 19 November 2026
MLC: tools
Performing right
The right to perform a song publicly, or to authorise others to. US law counts a performance as public where the public can attend, or when it is transmitted to the public. Royalty basics · US
USCCLIIC n.d.-b · USCCLIIC n.d.-a
Performing rights organisation (PRO)
A body that licenses public performance of non-dramatic musical works for their owners. US law calls it a performing rights society. Performance royalties · US
USCCLIIC n.d.-a
Phonorecord and digital phonorecord delivery (DPD)
A phonorecord is an object that holds recorded sound, such as vinyl or a CD. A DPD is a digital transmission that leaves the recipient with a specific copy: a permanent or limited download, or an on-demand stream. Mechanicals and the MLC · US
U.S. Copyright Office 2018 · 17 USC 115
Points (a producer's royalty)
The usual name for a producer's royalty on a record. Each point is one percentage point of whatever base the producer's agreement sets. Producer royalties · International
Publishing administrator
Industry name for a company that registers songs, collects their income and keeps the records on an owner's behalf, without becoming the owner. Terms vary by contract. Royalty basics · US, International
Sound recording (the master)
A work made by fixing a series of sounds, such as a recorded performance. In US law it excludes sounds that accompany a film or other audiovisual work. Royalty basics · US
USCCLIIC n.d.-a
Suspense account
An account where a collective holds royalties while the works in a usage report are still unidentified. A 2020 survey for the US Copyright Office lists checking it against past payouts among some societies' pre-payment steps. Unclaimed royalties · International
COSB 2020
Text and data mining (TDM)
Automated analysis of one or more digital works to extract information such as patterns, trends and correlations. In the EU, a commercial use is allowed only where the rightsholder has not reserved it. AI and music rights · Germany, France
GEMA 2023
The DLC (Digital Licensee Coordinator)
The non-profit that represents licensed digital services. It names a non-voting member of the MLC board and can take part in Copyright Royalty Board proceedings that set the assessment funding the MLC. Mechanicals and the MLC · US
Copyright Office: MMA FAQ · Copyright Office: MLC and DLC boards
The MLC (Mechanical Licensing Collective)
The non-profit the Copyright Office designated on 8 July 2019 to run the blanket license: it collects digital mechanicals, matches them to songs and owners, distributes them, and keeps the public database. Mechanicals and the MLC · US as of 19 November 2026
Copyright Office: MLC and DLC boards · Copyright Office: MMA FAQ
Training content summary
The overview of what content trained a general-purpose AI model, which EU rules require its provider to publish in the Commission's template format. AI and music rights · Germany, France
European Commission 2025b
Unclaimed accrued royalties
Money the MLC has collected but still cannot match to an owner or pay out when the legal minimum holding period ends. The MLC counts both unmatched and unclaimed amounts in it. Mechanicals and the MLC · US as of 19 November 2026
MLC: unclaimed accrued royalties · 17 USC 115
Unclaimed royalties
A loose word for royalties that no owner has collected yet. Meanings vary between collectors, and the MLC uses it both for its wider unclaimed accrued royalties and for one part of them. Read each collector's own definition. Unclaimed royalties · International
MLC: unclaimed accrued royalties
Unmatched royalties
Money collected for a use of a song that cannot be paid yet because the song, its owner or the owner's share is still unknown or unfound. In US law it means the owner "has not been identified or located". Unclaimed royalties · International, US
17 USC 115 · MLC: unclaimed accrued royalties
Work made for hire
A work whose author, in law, is the employer or commissioning party. Either an employee made it within the scope of the job, or it was commissioned for one of nine listed uses under a signed written agreement calling it for hire. Producer royalties · US
USCCLIIC n.d.-a · U.S. Copyright Office n.d.-c
Writer's share and publisher's share
Common industry names for the part of a song's income that goes to its writers and the part that goes to its publishers. See the note on how societies divide it. Royalty basics · International
