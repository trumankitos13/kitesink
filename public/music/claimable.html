Mechanical · US

The MLC

Unclaimed accrued US digital mechanicals under the blanket licence, 2021 usage onward

Who can claim
Songwriters, publishers and administrators whose songs earned US digital audio mechanicals that the MLC received but could not match or pay out.
How to claim
Use the MLC's tools to register songs, claim missing shares and propose matches for unmatched recordings. Money still unclaimed is later shared out by market share.
If nobody claims
The MLC plans to start market-share payouts in April 2027, beginning with usage from January 2021 and moving forward a month per payout. This is a start date, not a deadline.
Cost
Free to join, per the MLC

Mechanical · US

The MLC

Historical unmatched US streaming mechanicals from 2007 to 2020

Who can claim
Owners of musical works used in US streaming before 2021 whose use the services could not match to a copyright owner.
How to claim
Join the MLC, register your works, then use its claiming and matching tools to find missing shares and propose links to unmatched uses. Matched money is paid with interest.
If nobody claims
As of November 2026 the MLC says its market-share process for what stays unmatched has not begun. It gives no claim cut-off date on the page.
Cost
Free to join, per the MLC

Mechanical · US

The MLC

US digital mechanicals held for rightsholders who have not yet joined the MLC

Who can claim
Rightsholders who are not yet MLC Members and who find their own name, or the name they collect under, in the lookup.
How to claim
Search your name in the Missing Member Lookup. If it appears, sign up as an MLC Member to collect the digital audio mechanicals owed to you.
If nobody claims
The lookup leaves out historical unmatched royalties, so a hit shows only part of what may be owed. The page states no claim cut-off.
Cost
Free lookup and free to join

Mechanical · US

Copyright owners, usually publishers, paid MLC unclaimed money

The songwriter's share of any unclaimed accrued royalties a publisher receives from the MLC

Who can claim
Songwriters whose works a publisher licenses or administers for US digital mechanicals, or their authorised agents.
How to claim
When your publisher or administrator receives an MLC unclaimed distribution, ask for the payment or credit due to you and the usage behind it. If it is disputed, involve a music lawyer.
If nobody claims
By statute the share follows reported usage and is never under 50% of what the owner got for that songwriter's works or shares, whatever the contract says.
Cost
The statute sets no fee or claim process

Neighbouring rights · US

SoundExchange

US digital performance royalties for unregistered artists and sound recording owners

Who can claim
Unregistered recording artists, performers and sound recording owners whose recordings were played on US non-interactive services such as SiriusXM and Pandora.
How to claim
Use the search on SoundExchange's page to see if you appear on one of its unregistered lists. Then register for free and claim your recordings in SX Direct.
If nobody claims
The page says registration is required to be paid. It gives no time limit for claiming, so check with SoundExchange before assuming money is safe.
Cost
Free to register

Mechanical · Canada

CMRRA

Uses by online music services in Canada of recordings or work shares that CMRRA has not yet matched

Who can claim
Rightsholders who own or control musical works and hold an approved CMRRA Direct account. The page is written as a guide for publishers.
How to claim
Apply for a CMRRA Direct account (approval may need as long as 3 business days), open Unclaimed Works, match recordings to your works and add missing ownership shares.
If nobody claims
After CMRRA verifies the submission, its quarterly invoicing covers past and current use. The page gives no claim cut-off date.
Cost
No fee stated on the page

Neighbouring rights · UK

PPL

UK recorded-music royalties for performers missing from a recording's line-up

Who can claim
Performers on a recording in PPL's repertoire that was played in public or broadcast, if the performer's residence or citizenship, or where they recorded, qualifies.
How to claim
Join PPL as a performer and, if you are not on a track's line-up, claim in myPPL. PPL may ask for evidence. If the track itself is missing, ask its rightsholder to add it.
If nobody claims
The page states no claim deadline. It says the UK right to equitable remuneration through PPL runs 70 years from the recording being made or released.
Cost
No fee stated on the page
