# AI tools for music rights work: KiteSink

13 AI tools that touch music rights work, grouped by the job they do. For each: what it does, what you give it, and what its own terms say about who owns the output and whether your files train its models, in our words with a short quote and a link to the terms.

Accuracy figures, detection rates and claims that a model was trained only on licensed data are the vendor's own: KiteSink has not tested any tool, and a row that mentions one says so in its note.

Covers AI tools that touch music rights work whose own pages and terms could be opened and read, on the date each row gives. A tool not listed is not a judgment on it. Terms change, often: read the current terms before you upload anything, above all music you do not own outright or have not cleared with your co-writers and rightsholders.

## Metadata and matching

### Reprtoir

- [Reprtoir](https://reprtoir.com/) (Reprtoir SAS): Cloud workspace where labels and publishers keep track and work metadata, build release packages and process royalty statements; AI tagging is an add-on.
- You give it: Track and work metadata and files, royalty statements, imports from other services
- Outputs and your data: Users stay responsible for their content, and the terms give Reprtoir only the rights needed to run the service. They say nothing about model training or who owns AI tagging results, so ask the vendor.
- Their terms say: "The only rights granted to the Owner in relation to content provided by Users are those necessary to operate and maintain Reprtoir." ([the terms](https://reprtoir.com/terms))
- Note: Pricing lists Audio AI (auto-tagging, similarity search) as a paid-plan add-on. The terms, last updated 20 June 2020, do not mention AI.
- Access: Free, login needed. For: publishers and labels, administrators. Where: International. Checked 20 November 2026.

## AI-content detection and attribution

### AIMD AI Music Detector

- [AIMD AI Music Detector](https://www.ircamamplify.com/) (Ircam Amplify): Scores whether a track was made by an AI music generator, through an API, SDK or hosted platform. A score for human review, not a legal finding.
- You give it: Audio files, sent through an API, an SDK or the hosted platform
- Outputs and your data: The client keeps rights in submitted audio and in the results. Ircam says it will not train models on submitted audio or client-identifiable results unless the service order agrees, and deletes files after analysis.
- Their terms say: "Ircam Amplify shall not use submitted audio content or Client-identifiable analytical outputs to train or fine-tune any artificial intelligence or machine learning model" ([the terms](https://app.ircamamplify.io/terms-and-conditions-en.pdf))
- Note: The 99 percent accuracy and under 1 percent false-positive figures on its site are the vendor's own claim. Access is by request form.
- Access: Enterprise, through sales. For: publishers and labels, administrators. Where: International. Checked 20 November 2026.

### Sureel

- [Sureel](https://www.sureel.ai/) (Sureel Inc.): Lets rights owners set AI training and generation permissions per work, checks AI inputs and outputs against them, and reports which works shaped an output.
- You give it: Works and recordings registered with permission settings per work
- Outputs and your data: Assets stay the user's. Sureel takes a perpetual, irrevocable licence to use them to run and improve the service, training included; the opt-out covers its GenAI service only. Reports are for internal use unless Sureel agrees.
- Their terms say: "Sureel makes no claim of ownership to the Assets" ([the terms](https://www.sureel.ai/terms-of-use))
- Note: Attribution shares are the vendor's own claim; its terms say they may not be conclusive. The site says it is a Warner Music Group company.
- Access: Waitlist or invitation. For: songwriters, artists, publishers and labels, administrators. Where: International. Checked 20 November 2026.

## Licensed generators and output terms

### Suno

- [Suno](https://suno.com/) (Suno, Inc.): Text and lyrics to song generator with editing tools, stem separation and a voice feature built from the user's own recordings.
- You give it: Text prompts, lyrics, audio recordings and voice samples
- Outputs and your data: Pro and Premier: Suno assigns its rights in outputs, with no promise of copyright; commercial use needs a permitted download. Free tier: non-commercial only. Suno takes a perpetual licence to user content, including to improve its models.
- Their terms say: "If you are a user of the free or basic tier of the Service then you covenant and agree that you will only use such Outputs for your lawful, personal and non-commercial purposes." ([the terms](https://suno.com/terms))
- Note: Suno's help page says Suno owns free-plan songs; the terms only limit free-plan use to non-commercial. Terms effective 3 Sept 2026.
- Access: Free, login needed. For: songwriters, artists, producers. Where: International. Checked 20 November 2026.

### ElevenLabs Music

- [ElevenLabs Music](https://elevenlabs.io/music) (ElevenLabs): Generates songs from text prompts, with controls to regenerate one section and to write vocals in several languages.
- You give it: Text prompts and lyrics; optionally own tracks to make a style or vocal
- Outputs and your data: The user keeps rights in input and output. Free plan: no downloads, non-commercial use only. Plans below Enterprise Music exclude film, TV, radio and studio games. ElevenLabs takes a perpetual licence to improve services; training opt-out.
- Their terms say: "as between you and ElevenLabs, you retain all rights in and to your Output" ([the terms](https://elevenlabs.io/terms-of-use))
- Note: The claim that tracks are cleared for wide commercial use is the vendor's own. Its Music Terms (9 Oct 2026) set the rights plan by plan.
- Access: Free, login needed. For: songwriters, artists, producers. Where: International. Checked 20 November 2026.

### Beatoven.ai

- [Beatoven.ai](https://www.beatoven.ai/) (Beatoven Private Limited): Generates background music and sound effects from a text description, for use in videos, games and podcasts.
- You give it: A text description of the music wanted; the user's own video if submitted
- Outputs and your data: Beatoven, not the user, owns copyright in generated music. The user's licence covers use synced with their own project only: no streaming distribution, no use in other AI. Beatoven keeps a perpetual licence to submitted content.
- Their terms say: "Beatoven shall own and be the copyright owner of Your AI Music but you shall control the exercise of your rights pursuant to the License" ([the terms](https://www.beatoven.ai/tos))
- Note: Fairly Trained status is the vendor's own claim. Terms dated 5 June 2024. The FAQ allows sampling stems in remixes; the terms do not.
- Access: Paid. For: artists, producers. Where: International. Checked 20 November 2026.

## Catalogue and statement analysis

### ValuePunks

- [ValuePunks](https://www.valuepunks.com/) (Tribe Music Group AG): Estimates what a music catalogue is worth with a discounted-cash-flow model fed by revenue figures or royalty statements. An estimate, not an appraisal.
- You give it: Revenue per rights category, song lists or ISWCs, and royalty statements
- Outputs and your data: The user keeps rights in uploaded data. ValuePunks uses it to run the service and owns aggregated, de-identified data for benchmarking and model evaluation. Reports: own use and advisers; publishing needs consent unless the plan allows.
- Their terms say: "As between you and ValuePunks, you retain all right, title, and interest in and to your Customer Data." ([the terms](https://www.valuepunks.com/terms/))
- Note: Free for creators; Pro priced per valuation. Home page and terms never say AI; the terms say automated, model-based. Data room coming soon.
- Access: Free, login needed. For: songwriters, artists, publishers and labels, administrators. Where: International. Checked 20 November 2026.

## Voice and likeness

### Kits.ai

- [Kits.ai](https://kits.ai/) (Arpeggi, Inc.): Converts a vocal recording into another voice, trains custom voice models from the user's own recordings and separates vocals.
- You give it: Audio files of the user's own voice or a voice they have authority to use
- Outputs and your data: Custom voice models belong to the company; the user may use the model and its output commercially. Accounts opened since 2 Oct 2024 allow training on voice files and outputs unless opted out by email, for future files only.
- Their terms say: "we may use your Provided Voice Files (as defined below) and AI Model Output to train or otherwise improve our Services" ([the terms](https://kits.ai/terms-of-service))
- Note: Kits says its library voices are ethically licensed; that is its own claim. Each library voice has its own licence. Terms dated 2 Oct 2024.
- Access: Free, login needed. For: artists, producers. Where: International. Checked 20 November 2026.

### YouTube likeness detection

- [YouTube likeness detection](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16440338) (YouTube (Google)): Lets enrolled creators find uploads where their face appears AI-altered and ask for removal. Faces for now; voice detection is described as rolling out.
- You give it: Government ID, a short selfie video and optionally a voice sample
- Outputs and your data: Templates and ID data run the detection and are kept up to three years after last sign-in. Use for improving likeness models is optional and revocable. Data is not used to train Google generative AI without consent.
- Their terms say: "The data you provide to set up likeness detection isn’t used to train Google’s generative AI models without your consent." ([the terms](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16440338))
- Note: Needs a channel and a verified adult owner or manager. It matches likeness, not recordings; removals go through privacy complaints.
- Access: Free, login needed. For: artists. Where: International. Checked 20 November 2026.

## AI assistants: data-use terms

### Claude for Work and API

- [Claude for Work and API](https://claude.com/pricing) (Anthropic): Business and API plans of Anthropic's Claude assistant. Listed for the data-use terms that apply to music business documents.
- You give it: Prompts, documents and files the user chooses to send
- Outputs and your data: Customers keep their inputs and own the outputs; Anthropic assigns its rights, if any. Anthropic is barred from training on customer content. Its help page adds that feedback reports or data a customer opts in may be used.
- Their terms say: "Anthropic may not train models on Customer Content from Services." ([the terms](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/commercial-terms))
- Note: Commercial terms effective 17 June 2025; the data-use help article was dated 18 Aug 2026. Consumer plans differ; see the next row.
- Access: Paid. For: songwriters, artists, producers, publishers and labels, administrators. Where: International. Checked 20 November 2026.

### Claude Free, Pro and Max

- [Claude Free, Pro and Max](https://claude.com/pricing) (Anthropic): Consumer plans of Anthropic's Claude assistant. Listed for the data-use terms that apply when a person pastes their own contracts or statements into it.
- You give it: Prompts, documents and files the user chooses to send
- Outputs and your data: Users keep inputs and Anthropic assigns its rights in outputs. Chats may be used for model training unless the user opts out in account settings; flagged or rated chats can still be used. Terms effective 8 Oct 2025.
- Their terms say: "including training our models, unless you opt out of training through your account settings." ([the terms](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/consumer-terms))
- Note: The consumer help article (16 Mar 2026) frames training as a setting the user chooses; the terms say it happens unless the user opts out.
- Access: Free, login needed. For: songwriters, artists, producers, publishers and labels, administrators. Where: International. Checked 20 November 2026.

### Gemini in Google Workspace

- [Gemini in Google Workspace](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/generative-ai/generative-ai-in-google-workspace-privacy-hub) (Google): Gemini features inside Gmail, Docs, Drive and Meet, plus the Gemini app, for organisations on a qualifying Workspace edition.
- You give it: Prompts and the Workspace files, mail and meetings the user points it at
- Outputs and your data: Prompts count as customer data. Google says customer data is not used to tune its generative models unless the customer permits it. The hub page does not say who owns outputs.
- Their terms say: "Your content is not human reviewed or otherwise used for Generative AI model training outside your domain without permission." ([the terms](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/generative-ai/generative-ai-in-google-workspace-privacy-hub))
- Note: Hub last updated 6 Oct 2026. Applies only to qualifying Workspace editions; personal Gemini accounts have different terms (next row).
- Access: Paid. For: songwriters, artists, producers, publishers and labels, administrators. Where: International. Checked 20 November 2026.

### Gemini Apps (personal accounts)

- [Gemini Apps (personal accounts)](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961) (Google): Google's consumer Gemini chat apps. Listed for what happens to chats pasted in from a personal account.
- You give it: Prompts, uploaded files, audio and screen shares
- Outputs and your data: Chats can be reviewed by people and used to improve Google's models, with a setting to stop future chats being used for generative AI training. Temporary chats are not used. The page does not say who owns outputs.
- Their terms say: "You can stop your future chats from being reviewed to train generative AI models anytime in your Gemini History settings." ([the terms](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961))
- Note: Privacy hub last updated 19 Nov 2026. Google still uses some chat data in anonymised form whatever the setting says.
- Access: Free, login needed. For: songwriters, artists, producers, publishers and labels, administrators. Where: International. Checked 20 November 2026.

## How a row is checked

- Opened and checked: A tool is listed only after its own page and its terms were opened and read by two people. Each row carries the date.
- You give it: What the tool asks you to hand over: audio, stems, lyrics, statements, a catalogue export, a voice sample or a prompt.
- Outputs and your data: Our summary of the tool's terms on that date: who owns what it makes, and whether what you upload, or what it makes, may be used to train its models. Where its own pages disagree, the row says so.
- Their terms say: A short quote from the terms themselves, with a link to them, so you can check our summary against the tool's own words.
- Vendor claims: Accuracy figures, detection rates and claims that a model was trained only on licensed data are the vendor's own: KiteSink has not tested any tool, and a row that mentions one says so in its note.
- No ranking: Grouped by what each tool does, in our words. No ranking, no paid placement, no affiliate links.
