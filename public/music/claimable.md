# Claimable music royalties: KiteSink

7 places where music royalties can sit unclaimed, by holder and territory. Each row says who can claim, how, what it costs and what happens if nobody does, with the date it was checked and its sources.

Covers the holders whose own published pages or the law could be read and checked, in the territories this guide covers. A holder not listed may still hold money for you. Each row is dated: rules and amounts change, so check the holder's page before you act.

## The MLC: Unclaimed accrued US digital mechanicals under the blanket licence, 2021 usage onward

- Money: Mechanical · Where: US · For: songwriters, publishers and labels, administrators
- Who can claim: Songwriters, publishers and administrators whose songs earned US digital audio mechanicals that the MLC received but could not match or pay out.
- How to claim: Use the MLC's tools to register songs, claim missing shares and propose matches for unmatched recordings. Money still unclaimed is later shared out by market share.
- If nobody claims: The MLC plans to start market-share payouts in April 2027, beginning with usage from January 2021 and moving forward a month per payout. This is a start date, not a deadline.
- Cost: Free to join, per the MLC
- Link: [Unclaimed accrued royalties](https://www.themlc.com/unclaimed-accrued-royalties)
- Sourced (official) · As of 20 November 2026 · Sources: [MLC: unclaimed accrued royalties](https://www.themlc.com/unclaimed-accrued-royalties), [TMLC 2026b](https://www.themlc.com/news/the-mlc-holds-annual-membership-meeting-highlights-more-than-4.2-billion-in-total-royalties-distributed-and-announces-date-of-first-monthly-market-share-distribution), [MLC: Missing Member Lookup](https://www.themlc.com/missing-members-lookup)

## The MLC: Historical unmatched US streaming mechanicals from 2007 to 2020

- Money: Mechanical · Where: US · For: songwriters, publishers and labels, administrators
- Who can claim: Owners of musical works used in US streaming before 2021 whose use the services could not match to a copyright owner.
- How to claim: Join the MLC, register your works, then use its claiming and matching tools to find missing shares and propose links to unmatched uses. Matched money is paid with interest.
- If nobody claims: As of November 2026 the MLC says its market-share process for what stays unmatched has not begun. It gives no claim cut-off date on the page.
- Cost: Free to join, per the MLC
- Link: [Historical royalties](https://www.themlc.com/historical-royalties)
- Sourced (official) · As of 20 November 2026 · Sources: [MLC: historical royalties](https://www.themlc.com/historical-royalties), [MLC: Missing Member Lookup](https://www.themlc.com/missing-members-lookup)

## The MLC: US digital mechanicals held for rightsholders who have not yet joined the MLC

- Money: Mechanical · Where: US · For: songwriters, publishers and labels, administrators
- Who can claim: Rightsholders who are not yet MLC Members and who find their own name, or the name they collect under, in the lookup.
- How to claim: Search your name in the Missing Member Lookup. If it appears, sign up as an MLC Member to collect the digital audio mechanicals owed to you.
- If nobody claims: The lookup leaves out historical unmatched royalties, so a hit shows only part of what may be owed. The page states no claim cut-off.
- Cost: Free lookup and free to join
- Link: [Missing Member Lookup](https://www.themlc.com/missing-members-lookup)
- Sourced (official) · As of 20 November 2026 · Sources: [MLC: Missing Member Lookup](https://www.themlc.com/missing-members-lookup), [MLC: historical royalties](https://www.themlc.com/historical-royalties)

## Copyright owners, usually publishers, paid MLC unclaimed money: The songwriter's share of any unclaimed accrued royalties a publisher receives from the MLC

- Money: Mechanical · Where: US · For: songwriters, publishers and labels, administrators
- Who can claim: Songwriters whose works a publisher licenses or administers for US digital mechanicals, or their authorised agents.
- How to claim: When your publisher or administrator receives an MLC unclaimed distribution, ask for the payment or credit due to you and the usage behind it. If it is disputed, involve a music lawyer.
- If nobody claims: By statute the share follows reported usage and is never under 50% of what the owner got for that songwriter's works or shares, whatever the contract says.
- Cost: The statute sets no fee or claim process
- Link: [17 USC 115(d)(3)(J)(iv)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/17/115)
- Sourced (law) · As of 20 November 2026 · Sources: [17 USC 115](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/17/115)

## SoundExchange: US digital performance royalties for unregistered artists and sound recording owners

- Money: Neighbouring rights · Where: US · For: artists, producers, publishers and labels, administrators
- Who can claim: Unregistered recording artists, performers and sound recording owners whose recordings were played on US non-interactive services such as SiriusXM and Pandora.
- How to claim: Use the search on SoundExchange's page to see if you appear on one of its unregistered lists. Then register for free and claim your recordings in SX Direct.
- If nobody claims: The page says registration is required to be paid. It gives no time limit for claiming, so check with SoundExchange before assuming money is safe.
- Cost: Free to register
- Link: [Are you owed royalties?](https://www.soundexchange.com/what-we-do/for-artists-labels-and-producers/#are-you-owed-royalties)
- Sourced (official) · As of 20 November 2026 · Sources: [SoundExchange: artists, labels and producers](https://www.soundexchange.com/what-we-do/for-artists-labels-and-producers/)

## CMRRA: Uses by online music services in Canada of recordings or work shares that CMRRA has not yet matched

- Money: Mechanical · Where: Canada · For: publishers and labels, administrators, songwriters
- Who can claim: Rightsholders who own or control musical works and hold an approved CMRRA Direct account. The page is written as a guide for publishers.
- How to claim: Apply for a CMRRA Direct account (approval may need as long as 3 business days), open Unclaimed Works, match recordings to your works and add missing ownership shares.
- If nobody claims: After CMRRA verifies the submission, its quarterly invoicing covers past and current use. The page gives no claim cut-off date.
- Cost: No fee stated on the page
- Link: [Unclaimed Works Portal](https://cmrra.ca/unclaimed-works/)
- Sourced (official) · As of 20 November 2026 · Sources: [CMRRA Unclaimed Works Portal](https://cmrra.ca/unclaimed-works/)

## PPL: UK recorded-music royalties for performers missing from a recording's line-up

- Money: Neighbouring rights · Where: UK · For: artists, producers
- Who can claim: Performers on a recording in PPL's repertoire that was played in public or broadcast, if the performer's residence or citizenship, or where they recorded, qualifies.
- How to claim: Join PPL as a performer and, if you are not on a track's line-up, claim in myPPL. PPL may ask for evidence. If the track itself is missing, ask its rightsholder to add it.
- If nobody claims: The page states no claim deadline. It says the UK right to equitable remuneration through PPL runs 70 years from the recording being made or released.
- Cost: No fee stated on the page
- Link: [PPL performer claims](https://www.ppluk.com/royalties/uk-royalties/performers/)
- Sourced (official) · As of 20 November 2026 · Sources: [PPL: performers](https://www.ppluk.com/royalties/uk-royalties/performers/)
