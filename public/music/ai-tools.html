Metadata and matching

  • Reprtoir (opens in a new tab) Reprtoir SAS

    Cloud workspace where labels and publishers keep track and work metadata, build release packages and process royalty statements; AI tagging is an add-on.

    You give it
    Track and work metadata and files, royalty statements, imports from other services
    Outputs and your data
    Users stay responsible for their content, and the terms give Reprtoir only the rights needed to run the service. They say nothing about model training or who owns AI tagging results, so ask the vendor.
    Their terms say
    The only rights granted to the Owner in relation to content provided by Users are those necessary to operate and maintain Reprtoir. Read the terms of Reprtoir (opens in a new tab)

    Pricing lists Audio AI (auto-tagging, similarity search) as a paid-plan add-on. The terms, last updated 20 June 2020, do not mention AI.

AI-content detection and attribution

  • AIMD AI Music Detector (opens in a new tab) Ircam Amplify

    Scores whether a track was made by an AI music generator, through an API, SDK or hosted platform. A score for human review, not a legal finding.

    You give it
    Audio files, sent through an API, an SDK or the hosted platform
    Outputs and your data
    The client keeps rights in submitted audio and in the results. Ircam says it will not train models on submitted audio or client-identifiable results unless the service order agrees, and deletes files after analysis.
    Their terms say
    Ircam Amplify shall not use submitted audio content or Client-identifiable analytical outputs to train or fine-tune any artificial intelligence or machine learning model Read the terms of AIMD AI Music Detector (opens in a new tab)

    The 99 percent accuracy and under 1 percent false-positive figures on its site are the vendor's own claim. Access is by request form.

    Access Enterprise, through salesFor Publishers and labels and AdministratorsWhere InternationalChecked 20 November 2026

  • Sureel (opens in a new tab) Sureel Inc.

    Lets rights owners set AI training and generation permissions per work, checks AI inputs and outputs against them, and reports which works shaped an output.

    You give it
    Works and recordings registered with permission settings per work
    Outputs and your data
    Assets stay the user's. Sureel takes a perpetual, irrevocable licence to use them to run and improve the service, training included; the opt-out covers its GenAI service only. Reports are for internal use unless Sureel agrees.
    Their terms say
    Sureel makes no claim of ownership to the Assets Read the terms of Sureel (opens in a new tab)

    Attribution shares are the vendor's own claim; its terms say they may not be conclusive. The site says it is a Warner Music Group company.

Licensed generators and output terms

  • Suno (opens in a new tab) Suno, Inc.

    Text and lyrics to song generator with editing tools, stem separation and a voice feature built from the user's own recordings.

    You give it
    Text prompts, lyrics, audio recordings and voice samples
    Outputs and your data
    Pro and Premier: Suno assigns its rights in outputs, with no promise of copyright; commercial use needs a permitted download. Free tier: non-commercial only. Suno takes a perpetual licence to user content, including to improve its models.
    Their terms say
    If you are a user of the free or basic tier of the Service then you covenant and agree that you will only use such Outputs for your lawful, personal and non-commercial purposes. Read the terms of Suno (opens in a new tab)

    Suno's help page says Suno owns free-plan songs; the terms only limit free-plan use to non-commercial. Terms effective 3 Sept 2026.

    Access Free, login neededFor Songwriters, Artists and ProducersWhere InternationalChecked 20 November 2026

  • ElevenLabs Music (opens in a new tab) ElevenLabs

    Generates songs from text prompts, with controls to regenerate one section and to write vocals in several languages.

    You give it
    Text prompts and lyrics; optionally own tracks to make a style or vocal
    Outputs and your data
    The user keeps rights in input and output. Free plan: no downloads, non-commercial use only. Plans below Enterprise Music exclude film, TV, radio and studio games. ElevenLabs takes a perpetual licence to improve services; training opt-out.
    Their terms say
    as between you and ElevenLabs, you retain all rights in and to your Output Read the terms of ElevenLabs Music (opens in a new tab)

    The claim that tracks are cleared for wide commercial use is the vendor's own. Its Music Terms (9 Oct 2026) set the rights plan by plan.

    Access Free, login neededFor Songwriters, Artists and ProducersWhere InternationalChecked 20 November 2026

  • Beatoven.ai (opens in a new tab) Beatoven Private Limited

    Generates background music and sound effects from a text description, for use in videos, games and podcasts.

    You give it
    A text description of the music wanted; the user's own video if submitted
    Outputs and your data
    Beatoven, not the user, owns copyright in generated music. The user's licence covers use synced with their own project only: no streaming distribution, no use in other AI. Beatoven keeps a perpetual licence to submitted content.
    Their terms say
    Beatoven shall own and be the copyright owner of Your AI Music but you shall control the exercise of your rights pursuant to the License Read the terms of Beatoven.ai (opens in a new tab)

    Fairly Trained status is the vendor's own claim. Terms dated 5 June 2024. The FAQ allows sampling stems in remixes; the terms do not.

Catalogue and statement analysis

  • ValuePunks (opens in a new tab) Tribe Music Group AG

    Estimates what a music catalogue is worth with a discounted-cash-flow model fed by revenue figures or royalty statements. An estimate, not an appraisal.

    You give it
    Revenue per rights category, song lists or ISWCs, and royalty statements
    Outputs and your data
    The user keeps rights in uploaded data. ValuePunks uses it to run the service and owns aggregated, de-identified data for benchmarking and model evaluation. Reports: own use and advisers; publishing needs consent unless the plan allows.
    Their terms say
    As between you and ValuePunks, you retain all right, title, and interest in and to your Customer Data. Read the terms of ValuePunks (opens in a new tab)

    Free for creators; Pro priced per valuation. Home page and terms never say AI; the terms say automated, model-based. Data room coming soon.

    Access Free, login neededFor Songwriters, Artists, Publishers and labels and AdministratorsWhere InternationalChecked 20 November 2026

Voice and likeness

  • Kits.ai (opens in a new tab) Arpeggi, Inc.

    Converts a vocal recording into another voice, trains custom voice models from the user's own recordings and separates vocals.

    You give it
    Audio files of the user's own voice or a voice they have authority to use
    Outputs and your data
    Custom voice models belong to the company; the user may use the model and its output commercially. Accounts opened since 2 Oct 2024 allow training on voice files and outputs unless opted out by email, for future files only.
    Their terms say
    we may use your Provided Voice Files (as defined below) and AI Model Output to train or otherwise improve our Services Read the terms of Kits.ai (opens in a new tab)

    Kits says its library voices are ethically licensed; that is its own claim. Each library voice has its own licence. Terms dated 2 Oct 2024.

    Access Free, login neededFor Artists and ProducersWhere InternationalChecked 20 November 2026

  • YouTube likeness detection (opens in a new tab) YouTube (Google)

    Lets enrolled creators find uploads where their face appears AI-altered and ask for removal. Faces for now; voice detection is described as rolling out.

    You give it
    Government ID, a short selfie video and optionally a voice sample
    Outputs and your data
    Templates and ID data run the detection and are kept up to three years after last sign-in. Use for improving likeness models is optional and revocable. Data is not used to train Google generative AI without consent.
    Their terms say
    The data you provide to set up likeness detection isn’t used to train Google’s generative AI models without your consent. Read the terms of YouTube likeness detection (opens in a new tab)

    Needs a channel and a verified adult owner or manager. It matches likeness, not recordings; removals go through privacy complaints.

    Access Free, login neededFor ArtistsWhere InternationalChecked 20 November 2026

AI assistants: data-use terms

  • Claude for Work and API (opens in a new tab) Anthropic

    Business and API plans of Anthropic's Claude assistant. Listed for the data-use terms that apply to music business documents.

    You give it
    Prompts, documents and files the user chooses to send
    Outputs and your data
    Customers keep their inputs and own the outputs; Anthropic assigns its rights, if any. Anthropic is barred from training on customer content. Its help page adds that feedback reports or data a customer opts in may be used.
    Their terms say
    Anthropic may not train models on Customer Content from Services. Read the terms of Claude for Work and API (opens in a new tab)

    Commercial terms effective 17 June 2025; the data-use help article was dated 18 Aug 2026. Consumer plans differ; see the next row.

  • Claude Free, Pro and Max (opens in a new tab) Anthropic

    Consumer plans of Anthropic's Claude assistant. Listed for the data-use terms that apply when a person pastes their own contracts or statements into it.

    You give it
    Prompts, documents and files the user chooses to send
    Outputs and your data
    Users keep inputs and Anthropic assigns its rights in outputs. Chats may be used for model training unless the user opts out in account settings; flagged or rated chats can still be used. Terms effective 8 Oct 2025.
    Their terms say
    including training our models, unless you opt out of training through your account settings. Read the terms of Claude Free, Pro and Max (opens in a new tab)

    The consumer help article (16 Mar 2026) frames training as a setting the user chooses; the terms say it happens unless the user opts out.

  • Gemini in Google Workspace (opens in a new tab) Google

    Gemini features inside Gmail, Docs, Drive and Meet, plus the Gemini app, for organisations on a qualifying Workspace edition.

    You give it
    Prompts and the Workspace files, mail and meetings the user points it at
    Outputs and your data
    Prompts count as customer data. Google says customer data is not used to tune its generative models unless the customer permits it. The hub page does not say who owns outputs.
    Their terms say
    Your content is not human reviewed or otherwise used for Generative AI model training outside your domain without permission. Read the terms of Gemini in Google Workspace (opens in a new tab)

    Hub last updated 6 Oct 2026. Applies only to qualifying Workspace editions; personal Gemini accounts have different terms (next row).

  • Gemini Apps (personal accounts) (opens in a new tab) Google

    Google's consumer Gemini chat apps. Listed for what happens to chats pasted in from a personal account.

    You give it
    Prompts, uploaded files, audio and screen shares
    Outputs and your data
    Chats can be reviewed by people and used to improve Google's models, with a setting to stop future chats being used for generative AI training. Temporary chats are not used. The page does not say who owns outputs.
    Their terms say
    You can stop your future chats from being reviewed to train generative AI models anytime in your Gemini History settings. Read the terms of Gemini Apps (personal accounts) (opens in a new tab)

    Privacy hub last updated 19 Nov 2026. Google still uses some chat data in anonymised form whatever the setting says.
