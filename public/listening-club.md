# Album Listening Club

A monthly, in-person album listening club run by Truman Kitos-Lindsey. Three albums, one quiet afternoon. It is for the music-curious: welcoming, not gatekept. You do not need to know anything about music to sit in.

## Next session

- **When:** Saturday 16 January 2027, midday. This is the inaugural session of the 2027 season.
- **After that:** the last Saturday of every month.
- **How long:** about three hours, longer when the venue allows. Come and go as you like.
- **Where:** venues rotate. The venue and that month's three albums are posted in Discord about a week ahead.
- **Cost:** free. Bring a drink. No ticket and no RSVP.

## How an afternoon works

- That month's three albums play start to finish as the soundtrack for the session.
- About every fifteen minutes there is an optional prompt: composition, influence, emotional tone, production.
- At the end there is a Best Pick vote. The winner enters the Album Hall of Fame.
- Nothing is compulsory. Listening quietly counts as taking part.
