Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 22, 2026

Song of the Day is a quiet app, and it keeps quiet records. The absence of tracking is part of the product. Here is plainly what we hold, why we hold it, and how to make it disappear — written by a person, not a legal department.

What we collect

Three things, and only when they're needed:

  • User ID — An anonymous Supabase auth identifier, assigned the first time you open the app. It's how your collected songs stay yours. Anonymous by default — no name attached.
  • Email — Only if you Sign in with Apple or Google and choose to share it. Apple offers a private relay address by default, and that's perfectly fine with us.
  • Your content — The songs you collect, the songs you suggest to our editors, and your in-app preferences (theme, app icon). That's the lot.

What we don't collect

No advertising identifier (IDFA). No location. No contacts, photos, or microphone. No third-party analytics SDKs, no fingerprinting, no cross-app tracking of any kind. We do not build a profile of you, because we have nothing to sell and no one to sell it to.

How we use it

One purpose: app functionality. We sync your library across your devices when you sign in, and we show your editor the songs you suggested so they can actually read them. There is no second purpose.

Who we share it with

Supabase, which hosts our database. That's the entire list. No ad networks, no analytics vendors, no data brokers. We don't sell your data, and we never will.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. When you delete your account — Settings → Account → Delete account — everything is cascade-deleted from auth.users and bookmarks immediately. Not queued, not anonymized-and-kept. Gone.

Children

Song of the Day is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

California & GDPR

You have the right to access the data we hold about you and the right to have it deleted. The fastest route to deletion is the in-app button (Settings → Account → Delete account). For any other request, email privacy@songoftheday.app and a human will reply.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and bump the date at the top. No dark patterns, no buried diffs.
