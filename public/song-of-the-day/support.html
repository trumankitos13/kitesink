The short answers

Before you write.

These fix the things listeners run into most often.

My collected songs aren’t showing on another device.

Your library syncs only when you use the same Apple or Google sign-in on both devices. Open Settings → Account on each device and confirm the same sign-in method. Songs collected while signed out stay on that device until you sign in.

How do I change where songs play?

Open Settings → Listening service, then choose Apple Music, Spotify, or YouTube. Song of the Day does not host audio; the play button opens your chosen service.

The daily notification stopped arriving.

On your iPhone, open Settings → Notifications → Song of the Day and make sure Allow Notifications is on. If it is, check whether a Focus mode or Scheduled Summary is holding it. Reopening the app also refreshes the daily schedule.

Do I need an account to use the app?

No. You can read, listen, and collect songs without an account. Sign-in is only needed to keep your library in sync across devices.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. Your account and synced library are deleted immediately. For a data request you can also read the privacy policy or send a note below.

Can I suggest a song?

Yes. Use Suggest a song in the app. Add a sentence about why the track belongs in someone’s day—the context helps much more than a title alone.

Still stuck?

Tell me what happened.

Include what you tapped, what you expected, and what showed up instead. If the problem is tied to a particular song, include its title.
