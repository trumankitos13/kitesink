# Vantix

Vantix is a periodization app for lifters who have outgrown a spreadsheet, made by Truman Kitos-Lindsey at KiteSink. It is in progress and not open yet.

## What it does

- **You define the plan,** from the macrocycle down to the set. Weekly bands, effective-set maths and phase caps are computed continuously.
- **It normalizes your data.** Vantix connects to 500+ apps and accepts any export, and turns what they send into one record, with the raw data kept so everything can be re-derived.
- **An AI co-pilot proposes, you decide.** It never writes directly. It produces a change you accept, reject or edit row by row, and your hard rules decide whether a change can apply.
- **Weekly volume against your bands.** Each muscle group's stimulating reps (or effective sets) are read every week against the band you set, and shown as below, in or above it.
- **Three training views.** Volume (plan against done, by muscle group), Track (each planned line with what was actually done under it) and a session log.
