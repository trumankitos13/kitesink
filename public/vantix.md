# Vantix

Vantix is a periodization app for lifters who have outgrown a spreadsheet, made by Truman Kitos-Lindsey at KiteSink. It is in progress and not open yet.

## What it does

- **You define the plan,** from the macrocycle down to the set. Volume landmarks, effective-set maths and phase caps are computed continuously.
- **It normalizes your data.** Vantix connects to 500+ apps and accepts any export, and turns what they send into one record, with the raw data kept so everything can be re-derived.
- **An AI co-pilot proposes, you decide.** It never writes directly. It produces a change you accept, reject or edit row by row, and your hard rules decide whether a change can apply.
- **Weekly volume against your landmarks.** Each muscle group's sets are checked every week against the range you set.

## Volume landmarks, defined

- **MV, maintenance volume:** enough to keep what you have.
- **MEV, minimum effective volume:** the least that produces growth.
- **MAV, maximum adaptive volume:** where most of the progress happens.
- **MRV, maximum recoverable volume:** the most you can recover from.

These are coaching estimates that differ between people, not lab measurements.
