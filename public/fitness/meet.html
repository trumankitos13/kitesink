Meet day: your first competition, in order.

A timeline from signing up to the drive home and a bag list that remembers your ticks, then the guidance, numbers and tables behind them. Rules differ between federations: check yours.

Working notes from someone who trains, not a coach or a dietitian. General information, not medical advice.

In order

From sign-up to the drive home.

16 steps, then the bag list. Open a step to read it.

  1. Months out

    Meet day · Federation and entryChoose a federation: tested or untested sets who you lift againstTested federations drug test lifters; untested ones do not. The federation also sets your rules, classes and gear list.BeginnerRulebook

    USA Powerlifting limits competition to members who have not used performance-enhancing drugs for 36 months before the meet, and members can be tested at any time. The USPA offers tested meets only with national office approval, labels them 'Tested' and keeps their results separate from untested meets. IPF coaches in the warm-up and lifting area must acknowledge the IPF Anti-Doping Rules. Classes and gear lists differ, so read the rulebook of the federation you enter.

    • Find the federation's meet calendar and read its current rulebook before you enter.

    IPF 2026b · USA Powerlifting 2026 · USPA 2025

    Meet day · Federation and entryRegister, then pick a class you can reach by normal eatingJoin the federation, enter the meet by its deadline and choose a class near your normal weight.BeginnerRulebook

    USA Powerlifting memberships run from January 1 to December 31. USPA membership covers 12 months from the date of issue. Meets set their own entry forms, fees and deadlines. USPA requires ID and your membership card at weigh-in, and IPF weigh-in requires ID or a passport. Class limits differ between federations (see the class table), so check the one used by your meet.

    • Read the meet's entry form for deadlines, fees and the weigh-in schedule.
    • Choose the class you can hold with normal eating, not a cut.

    USA Powerlifting 2026 · USPA 2025 · IPF 2026b

  2. Weeks out

    Meet day · Classes and equipmentBuy legal gear early and train in itIn IPF rules you need a singlet, a T-shirt under it, shoes and shin-length socks for the deadlift; belt, sleeves and wraps are optional.BeginnerRulebook

    IPF limits: belt up to 10 cm (3.9 in) wide and 13 mm (0.5 in) thick; knee sleeves up to 7 mm thick and 30 cm (11.8 in) long; wrist wraps up to 1 m (39 in) long and 8 cm (3.1 in) wide; shoe soles up to 5 cm (2 in). Hats are not allowed. Belts, singlets and sleeves must come from makers on the IPF Approved List. USA Powerlifting uses the same sleeve and wrist wrap limits. Wear all of it in the last heavy sessions.

    • Check each item against your federation's approved list before you buy it.
    • Wear the full kit for your last heavy sessions.

    IPF 2026b · USA Powerlifting 2026 · Grgic 2017

    Meet day · Attempts and warm-upPlan nine attempts with your coach or a trusted friendPlan an opener you could lift easily on any day, a realistic second, and a hard but makeable third.BeginnerCoaching practice

    Elite classic lifters at IPF World Championships opened near 91% of their expected third attempt, then added about 5% for the second and about 3% for the third. Across 10,672 raw entries in Australian meets, squat openers averaged 92% of the lifter's best squat that day. Base your plan on your best recent single, not on a hope. Write all nine weights down with a lighter plan B for each lift, and round to 2.5 kg (5.5 lb) steps.

    • Use the attempt table below, and your best recent single, to fill in all nine weights.

    Travis 2021b · Howells 2022

  3. The week before

    Meet day · Training and prepTaper over the last week or two: less volume, a few heavy singlesSurvey data show a step taper with 41-50% less volume, the last heavy squat and deadlift 7-10 days out, and rest in the last days.IntermediateThin evidence

    In 364 North American raw powerlifters, a step taper over 7 to 10 days with 41-50% less volume was typical. The last heavy squat and deadlift came 7 to 10 days out, the last heavy bench under 7 days out, and full rest about 2.8 days before the meet. In a small trial of 19 athletes, 3 days off kept upper- and lower-body strength; 5 days off cost upper-body strength. A review found limited evidence that a short, heavy, low-volume session within 48 hours of competition can help.

    • Keep the lifts, cut the sets, and stop hard training about 3 days out.

    Travis 2021a · Travis 2022 · Harrison 2019

    Meet day · Training and prepConfirm the schedule, travel and food for the meetKnow your session, weigh-in time and flight, and have travel and meals sorted before the last days.BeginnerCoaching practice

    Find your session and flight on the meet schedule and confirm with the meet director. Book a bed close to the venue if the drive is long. Plan your food for the day before and the day of the meet, and pack the bag early. Squat and bench rack heights are written down at weigh-in, so measure yours in training.

    • Pack the bag a few days early using the checklist below.
    • Note your squat and bench rack heights and foot-block settings.

  4. The day before

    Meet day · Training and prepLay out gear, eat familiar food and sleep as normally as you canEat meals that you know sit well, drink fluids steadily and avoid new foods or heavy training.BeginnerCoaching practice

    Lay out singlet, T-shirt, socks, belt, shoes and ID. Skip new foods and alcohol. A poor night before a meet is common and a single night with little sleep had little effect on muscle strength in a systematic review of 17 studies, while several nights of restricted sleep could reduce force in compound lifts. Go to bed at your usual time and do not worry about one bad night.

    • Keep the day light: a short walk and easy mobility at most.
    • Go to bed at your normal time.

    Knowles 2018

  5. Weigh-in

    Meet day · Weigh-in and foodWeigh in about two hours before lifting, with ID and your opening attemptsIPF and USA Powerlifting weigh-ins run for 90 minutes and start two hours before lifting; the USPA can offer one up to 24 hours before.BeginnerRulebook

    IPF: weigh-in no earlier than two hours before the start for a category and lasts one and a half hours; bring ID or a passport; opening attempts are declared at weigh-in. USA Powerlifting: weigh-in starts two hours before lifting, lasts 90 minutes and ends 30 minutes before lifting. USPA: weigh-in lasts at least 90 minutes, with an optional early weigh-in up to 24 hours before lifting. If you miss class weight, you may return to the scale within the window.

    • Arrive early, bring ID and your written openers, and keep your gear bag with you.

    IPF 2026b · USA Powerlifting 2026 · USPA 2025

    Meet day · Classes and equipmentPass the equipment check and sign the rack height sheetReferees inspect and approve each item you plan to wear; an illegal item found later can disqualify you.BeginnerRulebook

    IPF: at least two referees inspect gear any time up to 30 minutes before your category starts; unclean or torn items are rejected, and over-length wraps can be cut and resubmitted. If you appear on the platform with an illegal item, you are disqualified. USA Powerlifting: every competitor has an equipment check, normally during the weigh-in period, with a 30-minute deadline before the session. Check squat and bench rack heights and sign the sheet.

    • Bring every item you plan to wear, including your belt and sleeves.
    • Sign the rack height sheet after checking each height.

    IPF 2026b · USA Powerlifting 2026

    Meet day · Weigh-in and foodEat and drink steadily after weigh-in, using food you knowAfter weigh-in, eat a familiar meal and sip fluids, then keep snacks nearby for the rest of the day.BeginnerThin evidence

    Eat normal, familiar foods and avoid very large or high-fibre meals just before the first squat. A systematic review of 49 carbohydrate studies found extra carbohydrate did not improve acute performance in 13 studies, and helped in six, mainly in fasted lifters or workouts of more than 10 sets per muscle group. A meet day is long, so steady eating and drinking matters more than any single food.

    • Eat a normal meal within an hour of weigh-in.
    • Sip fluids across the morning instead of drinking a large amount at once.

    Henselmans 2022

  6. The warm-up room

    Meet day · Attempts and warm-upTime your warm-up from the flight order, not the clockWork backwards from your opener: count the lifters ahead of you and finish your last warm-up a few lifters before your call.IntermediateCoaching practice

    Check the board for the flight order and watch how fast the platform moves. A common plan is a general warm-up, then 4 to 6 ramping sets on the bar, with the last warm-up about three to four lifters before your opener. Under IPF rules a single group gets 20 minutes between lifts, and each lifter has one minute to start after being called. Ask a coach or loader if you are unsure how many lifters are left.

    • Ask the warm-up room staff how many lifters are ahead of you every few minutes.
    • Be ready at the platform area when the lifter before you takes the bar.

    IPF 2026b

  7. On the platform

    Meet day · Platform and lightsSquat: wait for the command, reach depth, wait for 'rack'After unracking, stand still with knees locked and wait for 'Squat'; squat below parallel, stand up, then wait for 'Rack'.BeginnerRulebook

    IPF: step back, stand motionless with knees locked, and wait for the downward arm movement and 'Squat'. The top of the legs at the hip joint must go lower than the top of the knees, in one descent, then you rise with knees locked and wait for 'Rack'. If you are not in position after five seconds you hear 'Replace'. USA Powerlifting and the USPA use the same words. Foot movement, double bounces and missing a command can turn the lift down.

    • Settle, take a breath and wait for the full command before you move.

    IPF 2026b · USA Powerlifting 2026 · USPA 2025

    Meet day · Platform and lightsBench: wait for 'start', pause on the chest, wait for 'press'Lock the elbows and wait for 'Start'; lower the bar to your chest, hold still, wait for 'Press', then press and wait for 'Rack'.BeginnerRulebook

    IPF: head, shoulders and buttocks stay on the bench and feet stay flat on the floor. Grip with thumbs around the bar and hands no wider than 81 cm (32 in) between the forefingers. After 'Start', the bar goes to the chest or abdominal area and the underside of the elbows reaches level with or below the shoulders. Hold motionless until 'Press', then lock the arms and wait for 'Rack'. USA Powerlifting and the USPA use the same words.

    • Practise the pause on every training rep, with a count you can repeat.

    IPF 2026b · USA Powerlifting 2026 · USPA 2025

    Meet day · Platform and lightsDeadlift: pull to a full lockout and wait for 'down'Stand erect with knees locked and shoulders back and wait for 'Down'; then lower the bar with control.BeginnerRulebook

    IPF, USA Powerlifting and USPA use one command, 'Down', given once the bar is held motionless at a full lockout. Reasons for a turn-down include downward movement of the bar before lockout, supporting the bar on the thighs, lowering it before the command, losing control with both hands, and stepping. Foot movement after the command is not a fault. In IPF and USA Powerlifting meets you may change a third-attempt deadlift twice, unless you were already called to a bar loaded to your submitted weight.

    • Stand tall, hold the lockout for a beat, and wait for the command.

    IPF 2026b · USA Powerlifting 2026 · USPA 2025

  8. Between lifts

    Meet day · Weigh-in and foodBetween flights: small snacks, fluids, and caffeine only if triedEat and drink in small amounts between lifts and try any caffeine in training first.BeginnerSome support

    A meta-analysis of 10 strength studies found caffeine gave a small strength gain overall (standardised mean difference 0.20, 95% CI 0.03 to 0.36), clearer for the upper body than the lower body. Keep snacks small and familiar, and keep fluid steady. Caffeine affects people differently, so use only what you have used in training and stop if it causes jitters or an upset stomach.

    • Eat a small familiar snack and sip fluid after each discipline.
    • Use caffeine only in amounts you have tried in training.

    Grgic 2018

  9. Afterwards

    Meet day · After and bagAfter the meet: results, food, rest and a reviewCollect your results, eat and rest, and review what the attempts taught you before you plan the next cycle.BeginnerCoaching practice

    Ask for the score sheet and any video, and note which attempts were turned down and the reason shown on the cards. Eat and drink normally and take a few easy days before hard training starts. In tested federations you can be asked for a sample after lifting; follow the doping control officer's instructions. Write down what you would change about openers, warm-ups and food for the next meet.

    • Write a short review the next day, while details are fresh.

Federation and entryClasses, gear lists and drug testing differ between federationsIPF has 8 classes per sex; USA Powerlifting and the USPA list 12 each; check the one you will enter.BeginnerRulebook

IPF men's classes run from 59 kg to 120+ kg and women's from 47 kg to 84+ kg, plus a lowest sub-junior and junior class of 53 kg (men) or 43 kg (women). USA Powerlifting has 12 classes per sex (men 52 to 140+ kg, women 44 to 100+ kg). The USPA lists 12 (men 52 to 140.1+ kg, women 44 to 110+ kg). Gear must meet the federation's lists, and testing rules differ.

  • Check the class table, then the rulebook, of the federation that runs your meet.

IPF 2026b · USA Powerlifting 2026 · USPA 2025

Federation and entryTested meets use drug testing; untested meets may notA federation or meet name tells you which one it is; tested and untested results are kept separate.BeginnerRulebook

USA Powerlifting competes members who have not used performance-enhancing drugs for 36 months and can test members at any time. The USPA allows meet directors to run tested meets with approval, labels them 'Tested' and keeps results and records separate from untested meets. Which one you choose changes who you compete against and whether records count. This is not advice on medicines; ask a doctor if you take any prescription.

  • Check whether a meet is tested before you register.

USA Powerlifting 2026 · USPA 2025

Classes and equipmentArriving with gear you never checked against the rulebookGear that is not on the list, is too long or too thick, or is torn gets rejected at the equipment check.MistakeBeginnerRulebook

IPF inspectors reject unclean or torn items and gear outside the stated limits, such as a belt over 10 cm (3.9 in) wide or sleeves over 7 mm thick. An item found illegal on the platform after inspection can lead to disqualification. USA Powerlifting requires an equipment check for every competitor. Hats are not allowed on the platform in IPF rules.

  • Compare every item with the rulebook two weeks before the meet.

IPF 2026b · USA Powerlifting 2026

Training and prepRehearse the platform in training: commands, no mirror, a referee in frontA meet takes away the rack, the mirror and your usual gym. McDonald has lifters practise the commands for weeks and train some sets with those cues removed.BeginnerCoaching practice

This is McDonald's coaching practice, not a study result. He starts command practice about six weeks before a meet, three at the least, with a partner where the chief referee will sit giving the words and arm signals. On a lighter squat day the lifter faces away from the mirror, or covers it, to learn depth by feel, never in a rack that means walking backwards to re-rack. A weekly run-through of the three lifts in meet order also gets the bench used to following squats, which he thinks tire the shoulder girdle.

  • From about six weeks out, have a partner give the referee's words and arm signals on top sets (McDonald's practice).
  • On a lighter squat day, face away from the mirror or cover it (McDonald's practice).
  • Have someone who knows legal squat depth judge your squats honestly (McDonald's advice).

McDonald 2021a

Training and prepA light run-through of the three lifts a day or two out, not a full week offMcDonald's practice is a short session one or two days before the meet: squat, bench and deadlift in order, up to the last warm-up, under full commands.IntermediateCoaching practice

He uses it to keep lifters loose and sharp, to rehearse commands and the timed warm-up, and to finish on easy, clean lifts. His view is that raw lifters who take the whole final week off often feel flat. The loads stay light, so it fits the taper step's pattern of little hard work in the last days. A priming review that he links found limited evidence for short heavy (85% of 1RM or more) or ballistic sessions within 48 hours; the abstract names heavy or ballistic work, not a light session like this one.

  • Stop at the weight you plan as your last warm-up on meet day (McDonald's practice).

McDonald 2021a · Harrison 2019

Weigh-in and foodTrying new food or a big meal after weigh-inA large or unfamiliar meal after weigh-in can leave you full or ill when the first squat comes.MistakeBeginnerCoaching practice

Familiar food is the safe choice. A review of 49 carbohydrate studies found no clear acute strength gain from extra carbohydrate in most cases, so there is little reason to experiment on the day. Eat what has worked on a heavy training day, and keep portions moderate.

  • Rehearse your meet-day meals on a heavy training day.

Henselmans 2022

Attempts and warm-upYou can change an opener once; second attempts and bench or squat thirds are lockedYou submit each next weight within one minute of the lights; after that, changes are tightly limited.IntermediateRulebook

IPF: one change on the first attempt of each lift, higher or lower, up to three minutes before the first round starts for the first group. Second attempts and third attempts in squat and bench cannot be changed. In the third deadlift round you may change twice unless the bar has been called loaded. Weights are multiples of 2.5 kg (5.5 lb) and rise by at least 2.5 kg. If you miss, you may take that weight again, but only in the next round. USA Powerlifting uses the same structure.

  • Give your coach the written plan, the minimum increment, and who submits the card.

IPF 2026b · USA Powerlifting 2026

Attempts and warm-upCaffeine gave a small strength gain in upper-body liftsA meta-analysis of 10 strength studies found a small gain overall, clearer for upper-body strength.IntermediateSome support

Caffeine improved strength (standardised mean difference 0.20, 95% CI 0.03 to 0.36) and power. For upper-body strength the effect was 0.21 (95% CI 0.02 to 0.39); for lower-body strength it was 0.15 (95% CI -0.05 to 0.34), not clearly different from zero. Effects differ between people and the evidence in women is thin. Caffeine lasted longer in women on low-dose oral contraceptives, with a half-life of about 7.9 hours against 5.4 in non-users. Avoid trying it for the first time at a meet.

  • Try your planned dose in training before the meet.

Grgic 2018 · Abernethy 1985

Attempts and warm-upAim for nine good lifts and a smooth day, not a personal recordA first meet is practice for the whole day: weigh-in, warm-ups, commands and timing.BeginnerCoaching practice

Choose an opener that you could lift easily, take a realistic second and add a third only if the second felt solid. Good first-meet goals are hitting all commands, making nine good lifts and learning the flow of a meet. Records and totals come later. Bring a coach or friend who can read the flight order and keep the attempt plan.

  • Set two process goals, such as clean commands and on-time warm-ups.
Attempts and warm-upOpening with a weight that is nearly a maxAn opener that is hard to lift risks all three attempts and the total. At a first meet, open lighter, at about 85% of your best single or a little less.MistakeBeginnerCoaching practice

Elite classic lifters opened near 91% of their expected third attempt, and in 10,672 Australian raw entries squat openers averaged 92% of the best squat made that day. These data do not separate first meets. McDonald's rule for a first-timer is lighter: a weight you could squat for five clean reps on a bad day, about 80 to 85 percent, and lower still on the bench because of the pause and commands. A miss uses one of three tries, and a made opener means you cannot bomb out of that lift.

  • At a first meet, open with a weight you could use as your last warm-up.
  • With meet experience, choose an opener from your best recent single, then drop it a little if unsure.

Travis 2021b · McDonald 2021a · Howells 2022

Attempts and warm-upMissing your call because the warm-up ran longYou have one minute to start your attempt after the bar is called ready; a late start forfeits it.MistakeIntermediateCoaching practice

IPF: the lifter has one minute from the speaker's call to start the attempt, or the attempt is forfeited. Warm-ups that start too early leave you cold and bored; too late and you walk to the platform rushed. Count lifters ahead of you, use the board, and ask staff when unsure.

  • Finish the last warm-up several lifters before your opener.
  • Check rack height before you are called.

IPF 2026b

Platform and lightsThree referees show white or red lights, then cards give the reasonWhite means good lift and red means no lift; after the lights come up, cards show why a lift was turned down.BeginnerRulebook

In IPF rules each referee controls a white and a red light, and the lights come on together. A red, blue or yellow card (1, 2, 3) shows the reason for a failure. Common reasons: squat depth, double bounce or not standing erect, stepping and missed commands. Bench: bar not reaching the chest or elbows not low enough, downward movement or no lockout, heaving and missed commands. Deadlift: knees or shoulders not locked, supporting the bar on the thighs, lowering early or losing control.

  • Ask your coach to note the card colours so you can fix the cause.

IPF 2026b · USA Powerlifting 2026

Platform and lightsPlan your bench lift-off: who may hand you the bar depends on the federationUnder IPF rules the platform spotter/loaders may unrack the bar for you, and must bring it to arm's length. The USPA also allows your own lift-off person, who must then leave the platform.BeginnerRulebook

IPF rules let you take the bar out yourself or enlist the spotter/loaders, and an assisted lift-off must bring the bar to arm's length. USPA rules allow a personal lift-off person who steps away once it is done; only the official spotters may spot after that. Either way, you then lock the elbows and wait for 'Start'. Rules differ between federations and change, so check your federation's current rulebook. McDonald's view is that an unfamiliar hand-off that puts the bar in the wrong place or loosens your set-up is worse than none.

  • Before the attempt, tell the spotter your count and where you want the bar (McDonald's advice).
  • If you will unrack alone at the meet, unrack alone in training too (McDonald's practice).

IPF 2026b · USPA 2025 · McDonald 2021a

Platform and lightsMoving before the chief referee's commandUnder IPF and USPA rules, lowering the bench before 'Start', pressing before 'Press' or racking before 'Rack' fails the lift, however easily the weight went up.MistakeBeginnerRulebook

Both rulebooks list failure to observe the referee's signals as a cause for turning down a bench press, and for the start or finish of a squat. Rules differ between federations and change, so check your federation's current rulebook. McDonald's view is that these are the commonest first-meet misses on the bench: nerves make a short pause feel long, and gym habits such as touch-and-go reps or racking straight from lockout carry onto the platform. The commands table lists each word.

  • At lockout, hold the bar still and wait for 'Rack' before moving toward the hooks.
