Grading

How entries are graded.

Every entry carries one of these five labels. It says how much stands behind the point.

Well supported
Meta-analyses, position stands, guidelines or several consistent trials.
Rulebook
A federation rule or an official guideline value.
Some support
A few trials or consistent observational data.
Thin evidence
One or two small studies, or mixed findings.
Coaching practice
Common coaching convention. No direct study.
