# Fitness sources, grading and corrections: KiteSink

Where the KiteSink fitness field guide comes from: how entries are graded, what has been corrected, and all 299 sources. Each source was checked against its record.

## How the entries are graded

- Well supported: Meta-analyses, position stands, guidelines or several consistent trials.
- Rulebook: A federation rule or an official guideline value.
- Some support: A few trials or consistent observational data.
- Thin evidence: One or two small studies, or mixed findings.
- Coaching practice: Common coaching convention. No direct study.

## Corrections

What changed after an entry was published, newest first.

- **2026-11-14 · Does spot reduction work?**: Reworded after reading the trial's full text: abdominal work replaced part of the running, and the two programmes were designed to use the same energy. The verdict did not change. (https://kitesink.com/fitness/claims#claim-spot-reduction)
- **2026-11-14 · Can your body only use 30 grams of protein per meal?**: Added that the trial used milk protein, which is mostly slow-digesting casein. The verdict did not change. (https://kitesink.com/fitness/claims#claim-protein-meal-cap)
- **2026-11-14 · 1RM and load calculator**: The estimate became the average of the Epley and Brzycki equations for sets of up to ten reps; it was Epley alone. 100 kg × 5 now reads 115 kg, not 117. (https://kitesink.com/fitness#loads)
