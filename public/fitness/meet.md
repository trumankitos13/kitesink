# Meet day: KiteSink fitness field guide

Your first competition, in order. Each entry gives the point, the reasoning, what to do, how well supported it is, and its sources.

## Guidance

### Classes, gear lists and drug testing differ between federations

**IPF has 8 classes per sex; USA Powerlifting and the USPA list 12 each; check the one you will enter.**

IPF men's classes run from 59 kg to 120+ kg and women's from 47 kg to 84+ kg, plus a lowest sub-junior and junior class of 53 kg (men) or 43 kg (women). USA Powerlifting has 12 classes per sex (men 52 to 140+ kg, women 44 to 100+ kg). The USPA lists 12 (men 52 to 140.1+ kg, women 44 to 110+ kg). Gear must meet the federation's lists, and testing rules differ.

- Check the class table, then the rulebook, of the federation that runs your meet.

Federation and entry · Beginner · Rulebook · Sources: [IPF 2026b](https://www.powerlifting.sport/fileadmin/ipf/data/rules/technical-rules/english/2026_IPF_Technical_Rulebook__effective_01_March_2026__v3.pdf), [USA Powerlifting 2026](https://www.usapowerlifting.com/technical/), [USPA 2025](https://uspa.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/USPA-Rulebook-March-24th-2025.pdf)

### Three referees show white or red lights, then cards give the reason

**White means good lift and red means no lift; after the lights come up, cards show why a lift was turned down.**

In IPF rules each referee controls a white and a red light, and the lights come on together. A red, blue or yellow card (1, 2, 3) shows the reason for a failure. Common reasons: squat depth, double bounce or not standing erect, stepping and missed commands. Bench: bar not reaching the chest or elbows not low enough, downward movement or no lockout, heaving and missed commands. Deadlift: knees or shoulders not locked, supporting the bar on the thighs, lowering early or losing control.

- Ask your coach to note the card colours so you can fix the cause.

Platform and lights · Beginner · Rulebook · Sources: [IPF 2026b](https://www.powerlifting.sport/fileadmin/ipf/data/rules/technical-rules/english/2026_IPF_Technical_Rulebook__effective_01_March_2026__v3.pdf), [USA Powerlifting 2026](https://www.usapowerlifting.com/technical/)

### You can change an opener once; second attempts and bench or squat thirds are locked

**You submit each next weight within one minute of the lights; after that, changes are tightly limited.**

IPF: one change on the first attempt of each lift, higher or lower, up to three minutes before the first round starts for the first group. Second attempts and third attempts in squat and bench cannot be changed. In the third deadlift round you may change twice unless the bar has been called loaded. Weights are multiples of 2.5 kg (5.5 lb) with at least 2.5 kg between attempts. If you miss, you do not follow yourself; the next attempt comes in the next round. USA Powerlifting uses the same structure.

- Give your coach the written plan, the minimum increment, and who submits the card.

### Caffeine gave a small strength gain in upper-body lifts

**A meta-analysis of 10 strength studies found a small gain overall, clearer for upper-body strength.**

Caffeine improved strength (standardised mean difference 0.20, 95% CI 0.03 to 0.36) and power. For upper-body strength the effect was 0.21 (95% CI 0.02 to 0.39); for lower-body strength it was 0.15 (95% CI -0.05 to 0.34), not clearly different from zero. Effects differ between people and the evidence in women is thin. Avoid trying it for the first time at a meet.

- Try your planned dose in training before the meet.

Attempts and warm-up · Intermediate · Some support · Sources: [Grgic 2018](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12970-018-0216-0)

### Aim for nine good lifts and a smooth day, not a personal record

**A first meet is practice for the whole day: weigh-in, warm-ups, commands and timing.**

Choose an opener that you could lift easily, take a realistic second and add a third only if the second felt solid. Good first-meet goals are hitting all commands, making nine good lifts and learning the flow of a meet. Records and totals come later. Bring a coach or friend who can read the flight order and keep the attempt plan.

- Set two process goals, such as clean commands and on-time warm-ups.

Attempts and warm-up · Beginner · Coaching practice

### Tested meets use drug testing; untested meets may not

**A federation or meet name tells you which one it is; tested and untested results are kept separate.**

USA Powerlifting competes members who have not used performance-enhancing drugs for 36 months and can test members at any time. The USPA allows meet directors to run tested meets with approval, labels them 'Tested' and keeps results and records separate from untested meets. Which one you choose changes who you compete against and whether records count. This is not advice on medicines; ask a doctor if you take any prescription.

- Check whether a meet is tested before you register.

## Common mistakes

### Opening with a weight that is nearly a max

**An opener that is hard to lift risks all three attempts and a total; keep it around 88-92% of a current best single.**

Elite classic lifters opened near 91% of their expected third attempt. A heavy opener burns nerves and energy, and a miss uses one of three tries. First-time lifters often over-pick because the day feels exciting. Pick a weight you could lift for a clean double on a normal day, then build.

- Choose an opener from your best recent single, then drop it a little if unsure.

Attempts and warm-up · Beginner · Coaching practice · Sources: [Travis 2021b](https://doi.org/10.1177/0031512520967608)

### Arriving with gear you never checked against the rulebook

**Gear that is not on the list, is too long or too thick, or is torn gets rejected at the equipment check.**

IPF inspectors reject unclean or torn items and gear outside the stated limits, such as a belt over 10 cm (3.9 in) wide or sleeves over 7 mm thick. An item found illegal on the platform after inspection can lead to disqualification. USA Powerlifting requires an equipment check for every competitor. Hats are not allowed on the platform in IPF rules.

- Compare every item with the rulebook two weeks before the meet.

Classes and equipment · Beginner · Rulebook · Sources: [IPF 2026b](https://www.powerlifting.sport/fileadmin/ipf/data/rules/technical-rules/english/2026_IPF_Technical_Rulebook__effective_01_March_2026__v3.pdf), [USA Powerlifting 2026](https://www.usapowerlifting.com/technical/)

### Missing your call because the warm-up ran long

**You have one minute to start your attempt after the bar is called ready; a late start forfeits it.**

IPF: the lifter has one minute from the speaker's call to start the attempt, or the attempt is forfeited. Warm-ups that start too early leave you cold and bored; too late and you walk to the platform rushed. Count lifters ahead of you, use the board, and ask staff when unsure.

- Finish the last warm-up several lifters before your opener.
- Check rack height before you are called.

Attempts and warm-up · Intermediate · Coaching practice · Sources: [IPF 2026b](https://www.powerlifting.sport/fileadmin/ipf/data/rules/technical-rules/english/2026_IPF_Technical_Rulebook__effective_01_March_2026__v3.pdf)

### Trying new food or a big meal after weigh-in

**A large or unfamiliar meal after weigh-in can leave you full or ill when the first squat comes.**

Familiar food is the safe choice. A review of 49 carbohydrate studies found no clear acute strength gain from extra carbohydrate in most cases, so there is little reason to experiment on the day. Eat what has worked on a heavy training day, and keep portions moderate.

- Rehearse your meet-day meals on a heavy training day.

## In order

### Choose a federation: tested or untested sets who you lift against

**Tested federations drug test lifters; untested ones do not. The federation also sets your rules, classes and gear list.**

USA Powerlifting limits competition to members who have not used performance-enhancing drugs for 36 months before the meet, and members can be tested at any time. The USPA offers tested meets only with national office approval, labels them 'Tested' and keeps their results separate from untested meets. IPF coaches in the warm-up and lifting area must acknowledge the IPF Anti-Doping Rules. Classes and gear lists differ, so read the rulebook of the federation you enter.

- Find the federation's meet calendar and read its current rulebook before you enter.

Months out · Beginner · Rulebook · Sources: [IPF 2026b](https://www.powerlifting.sport/fileadmin/ipf/data/rules/technical-rules/english/2026_IPF_Technical_Rulebook__effective_01_March_2026__v3.pdf), [USA Powerlifting 2026](https://www.usapowerlifting.com/technical/), [USPA 2025](https://uspa.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/USPA-Rulebook-March-24th-2025.pdf)

### Register, then pick a class you can reach by normal eating

**Join the federation, enter the meet by its deadline and choose a class near your normal weight.**

USA Powerlifting memberships run from January 1 to December 31. USPA membership covers 12 months from the date of issue. Meets set their own entry forms, fees and deadlines. USPA requires ID and your membership card at weigh-in, and IPF weigh-in requires ID or a passport. Class limits differ between federations (see the class table), so check the one used by your meet.

- Read the meet's entry form for deadlines, fees and the weigh-in schedule.
- Choose the class you can hold with normal eating, not a cut.

Months out · Beginner · Rulebook · Sources: [USA Powerlifting 2026](https://www.usapowerlifting.com/technical/), [USPA 2025](https://uspa.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/USPA-Rulebook-March-24th-2025.pdf), [IPF 2026b](https://www.powerlifting.sport/fileadmin/ipf/data/rules/technical-rules/english/2026_IPF_Technical_Rulebook__effective_01_March_2026__v3.pdf)

### Buy legal gear early and train in it

**In IPF rules you need a singlet, a T-shirt under it, shoes and shin-length socks for the deadlift; belt, sleeves and wraps are optional.**

IPF limits: belt up to 10 cm (3.9 in) wide and 13 mm (0.5 in) thick; knee sleeves up to 7 mm thick and 30 cm (11.8 in) long; wrist wraps up to 1 m (39 in) long and 8 cm (3.1 in) wide; shoe soles up to 5 cm (2 in). Hats are not allowed. Belts, singlets and sleeves must come from makers on the IPF Approved List. USA Powerlifting uses the same sleeve and wrist wrap limits. Wear all of it in the last heavy sessions.

- Check each item against your federation's approved list before you buy it.
- Wear the full kit for your last heavy sessions.

Weeks out · Beginner · Rulebook · Sources: [IPF 2026b](https://www.powerlifting.sport/fileadmin/ipf/data/rules/technical-rules/english/2026_IPF_Technical_Rulebook__effective_01_March_2026__v3.pdf), [USA Powerlifting 2026](https://www.usapowerlifting.com/technical/), [Grgic 2017](https://doi.org/10.1519/JSC.0000000000001699)

### Plan nine attempts with your coach or a trusted friend

**Plan an opener you could lift easily on any day, a realistic second, and a hard but makeable third.**

Elite classic lifters at IPF World Championships opened near 91% of their expected third attempt, then added about 5% for the second and about 3% for the third. Base your plan on your best recent single, not on a hope. Write all nine weights down with a lighter plan B for each lift, and round to 2.5 kg (5.5 lb) steps.

- Use the attempt table below, and your best recent single, to fill in all nine weights.

Weeks out · Beginner · Coaching practice · Sources: [Travis 2021b](https://doi.org/10.1177/0031512520967608)

### Taper over the last week or two: less volume, a few heavy singles

**Survey data show a step taper with 41-50% less volume, the last heavy squat and deadlift 7-10 days out, and rest in the last days.**

In 364 North American raw powerlifters, a step taper over 7 to 10 days with 41-50% less volume was typical. The last heavy squat and deadlift came 7 to 10 days out, the last heavy bench under 7 days out, and full rest about 2.8 days before the meet. In a small trial of 19 athletes, 3 days off kept upper- and lower-body strength; 5 days off cost upper-body strength.

- Keep the lifts, cut the sets, and stop hard training about 3 days out.

The week before · Intermediate · Thin evidence · Sources: [Travis 2021a](https://doi.org/10.1519/JSC.0000000000004177), [Travis 2022](https://doi.org/10.1519/JSC.0000000000004183)

### Confirm the schedule, travel and food for the meet

**Know your session, weigh-in time and flight, and have travel and meals sorted before the last days.**

Find your session and flight on the meet schedule and confirm with the meet director. Book a bed close to the venue if the drive is long. Plan your food for the day before and the day of the meet, and pack the bag early. Squat and bench rack heights are written down at weigh-in, so measure yours in training.

- Pack the bag a few days early using the checklist below.
- Note your squat and bench rack heights and foot-block settings.

The week before · Beginner · Coaching practice

### Lay out gear, eat familiar food and sleep as normally as you can

**Eat meals that you know sit well, drink fluids steadily and avoid new foods or heavy training.**

Lay out singlet, T-shirt, socks, belt, shoes and ID. Skip new foods and alcohol. A poor night before a meet is common and a single night with little sleep had little effect on muscle strength in a systematic review of 17 studies, while several nights of restricted sleep could reduce force in compound lifts. Go to bed at your usual time and do not worry about one bad night.

- Keep the day light: a short walk and easy mobility at most.
- Go to bed at your normal time.

The day before · Beginner · Coaching practice · Sources: [Knowles 2018](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jsams.2018.01.012)

### Weigh in about two hours before lifting, with ID and your opening attempts

**IPF and USA Powerlifting weigh-ins run for 90 minutes and start two hours before lifting; the USPA can offer one up to 24 hours before.**

IPF: weigh-in no earlier than two hours before the start for a category and lasts one and a half hours; bring ID or a passport; opening attempts are declared at weigh-in. USA Powerlifting: weigh-in starts two hours before lifting, lasts 90 minutes and ends 30 minutes before lifting. USPA: weigh-in lasts at least 90 minutes, with an optional early weigh-in up to 24 hours before lifting. If you miss class weight, you may return to the scale within the window.

- Arrive early, bring ID and your written openers, and keep your gear bag with you.

Weigh-in · Beginner · Rulebook · Sources: [IPF 2026b](https://www.powerlifting.sport/fileadmin/ipf/data/rules/technical-rules/english/2026_IPF_Technical_Rulebook__effective_01_March_2026__v3.pdf), [USA Powerlifting 2026](https://www.usapowerlifting.com/technical/), [USPA 2025](https://uspa.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/USPA-Rulebook-March-24th-2025.pdf)

### Pass the equipment check and sign the rack height sheet

**Referees inspect and approve each item you plan to wear; an illegal item found later can disqualify you.**

IPF: at least two referees inspect gear any time up to 30 minutes before your category starts; unclean or torn items are rejected, and over-length wraps can be cut and resubmitted. If you appear on the platform with an illegal item, you are disqualified. USA Powerlifting: every competitor has an equipment check, normally during the weigh-in period, with a 30-minute deadline before the session. Check squat and bench rack heights and sign the sheet.

- Bring every item you plan to wear, including your belt and sleeves.
- Sign the rack height sheet after checking each height.

Weigh-in · Beginner · Rulebook · Sources: [IPF 2026b](https://www.powerlifting.sport/fileadmin/ipf/data/rules/technical-rules/english/2026_IPF_Technical_Rulebook__effective_01_March_2026__v3.pdf), [USA Powerlifting 2026](https://www.usapowerlifting.com/technical/)

### Eat and drink steadily after weigh-in, using food you know

**After weigh-in, eat a familiar meal and sip fluids, then keep snacks nearby for the rest of the day.**

Eat normal, familiar foods and avoid very large or high-fibre meals just before the first squat. A systematic review of 49 carbohydrate studies found extra carbohydrate did not improve acute performance in 13 studies, and helped in six, mainly in fasted lifters or workouts of more than 10 sets per muscle group. A meet day is long, so steady eating and drinking matters more than any single food.

- Eat a normal meal within an hour of weigh-in.
- Sip fluids across the morning instead of drinking a large amount at once.

Weigh-in · Beginner · Thin evidence · Sources: [Henselmans 2022](https://doi.org/10.3390/nu14040856)

### Time your warm-up from the flight order, not the clock

**Work backwards from your opener: count the lifters ahead of you and finish your last warm-up a few lifters before your call.**

Check the board for the flight order and watch how fast the platform moves. A common plan is a general warm-up, then 4 to 6 ramping sets on the bar, with the last warm-up about three to four lifters before your opener. Under IPF rules a single group gets 20 minutes between lifts, and each lifter has one minute to start after being called. Ask a coach or loader if you are unsure how many lifters are left.

- Ask the warm-up room staff how many lifters are ahead of you every few minutes.
- Be ready at the platform area when the lifter before you takes the bar.

The warm-up room · Intermediate · Coaching practice · Sources: [IPF 2026b](https://www.powerlifting.sport/fileadmin/ipf/data/rules/technical-rules/english/2026_IPF_Technical_Rulebook__effective_01_March_2026__v3.pdf)

### Squat: wait for the command, reach depth, wait for 'rack'

**After unracking, stand still with knees locked and wait for 'Squat'; squat below parallel, stand up, then wait for 'Rack'.**

IPF: step back, stand motionless with knees locked, and wait for the downward arm movement and 'Squat'. The top of the legs at the hip joint must go lower than the top of the knees, in one descent, then you rise with knees locked and wait for 'Rack'. If you are not in position after five seconds you hear 'Replace'. USA Powerlifting and the USPA use the same words. Foot movement, double bounces and missing a command can turn the lift down.

- Settle, take a breath and wait for the full command before you move.

On the platform · Beginner · Rulebook · Sources: [IPF 2026b](https://www.powerlifting.sport/fileadmin/ipf/data/rules/technical-rules/english/2026_IPF_Technical_Rulebook__effective_01_March_2026__v3.pdf), [USA Powerlifting 2026](https://www.usapowerlifting.com/technical/), [USPA 2025](https://uspa.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/USPA-Rulebook-March-24th-2025.pdf)

### Bench: wait for 'start', pause on the chest, wait for 'press'

**Lock the elbows and wait for 'Start'; lower the bar to your chest, hold still, wait for 'Press', then press and wait for 'Rack'.**

IPF: head, shoulders and buttocks stay on the bench and feet stay flat on the floor. Grip with thumbs around the bar and hands no wider than 81 cm (32 in) between the forefingers. After 'Start', the bar goes to the chest or abdominal area and the underside of the elbows reaches level with or below the shoulders. Hold motionless until 'Press', then lock the arms and wait for 'Rack'. USA Powerlifting and the USPA use the same words.

- Practise the pause on every training rep, with a count you can repeat.

On the platform · Beginner · Rulebook · Sources: [IPF 2026b](https://www.powerlifting.sport/fileadmin/ipf/data/rules/technical-rules/english/2026_IPF_Technical_Rulebook__effective_01_March_2026__v3.pdf), [USA Powerlifting 2026](https://www.usapowerlifting.com/technical/), [USPA 2025](https://uspa.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/USPA-Rulebook-March-24th-2025.pdf)

### Deadlift: pull to a full lockout and wait for 'down'

**Stand erect with knees locked and shoulders back and wait for 'Down'; then lower the bar with control.**

IPF, USA Powerlifting and USPA use one command, 'Down', given once the bar is held motionless at a full lockout. Reasons for a turn-down include downward movement of the bar before lockout, supporting the bar on the thighs, lowering it before the command, losing control with both hands, and stepping. Foot movement after the command is not a fault. In IPF and USA Powerlifting meets you may change a third-attempt deadlift twice, unless you were already called to a bar loaded to your submitted weight.

- Stand tall, hold the lockout for a beat, and wait for the command.

On the platform · Beginner · Rulebook · Sources: [IPF 2026b](https://www.powerlifting.sport/fileadmin/ipf/data/rules/technical-rules/english/2026_IPF_Technical_Rulebook__effective_01_March_2026__v3.pdf), [USA Powerlifting 2026](https://www.usapowerlifting.com/technical/), [USPA 2025](https://uspa.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/USPA-Rulebook-March-24th-2025.pdf)

### Between flights: small snacks, fluids, and caffeine only if tried

**Eat and drink in small amounts between lifts and try any caffeine in training first.**

A meta-analysis of 10 strength studies found caffeine gave a small strength gain overall (standardised mean difference 0.20, 95% CI 0.03 to 0.36), clearer for the upper body than the lower body. Keep snacks small and familiar, and keep fluid steady. Caffeine affects people differently, so use only what you have used in training and stop if it causes jitters or an upset stomach.

- Eat a small familiar snack and sip fluid after each discipline.
- Use caffeine only in amounts you have tried in training.

Between lifts · Beginner · Some support · Sources: [Grgic 2018](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12970-018-0216-0)

### After the meet: results, food, rest and a review

**Collect your results, eat and rest, and review what the attempts taught you before you plan the next cycle.**

Ask for the score sheet and any video, and note which attempts were turned down and the reason shown on the cards. Eat and drink normally and take a few easy days before hard training starts. In tested federations you can be asked for a sample after lifting; follow the doping control officer's instructions. Write down what you would change about openers, warm-ups and food for the next meet.

- Write a short review the next day, while details are fresh.

## Platform commands for each lift

Commands as given by the chief referee in IPF, USA Powerlifting and USPA rules; wait for each one.

| Lift | Commands in order | What you do first | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Squat | 'Squat', then 'Rack' | Stand erect, knees locked, motionless | Depth: top of legs at the hip below the top of the knees |
| Bench press | 'Start', 'Press', 'Rack' | Arms locked and still for 'Start'; bar still on chest for 'Press' | Elbows level with or below the shoulders at the chest |
| Deadlift | 'Down' | Stand erect, knees locked, shoulders back | One command, given once the lockout is held |
| Squat or bench | 'Replace' | Fix the reason the referee gives you | IPF: if not in position after five seconds |

## Attempt selection ranges for a first meet

Typical ranges as a share of a current best single, with an example for a lifter whose best single is 200 kg (441 lb).

| Attempt | % of current best single | Example at 200 kg (441 lb) | Aim |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opener | 88-92% | 176-184 kg (388-406 lb) | A weight you could lift easily on any day |
| Second | 94-97.5% | 188-195 kg (414-430 lb) | A solid, realistic lift |
| Third | 98-102.5% | 196-205 kg (432-452 lb) | A hard but makeable attempt |

## Meet bag checklist

Pack in the days before the meet, and check the rules of your federation for each item.

**Required to lift**

- Singlet
- T-shirt to wear under the singlet
- Shin-length socks for the deadlift
- Lifting shoes or deadlift slippers
- Photo ID or passport
- Federation membership card

**Optional gear (if your federation allows)**

- Belt (IPF: up to 10 cm / 3.9 in wide, 13 mm thick)

- Wrist wraps (up to 1 m / 39 in long, 8 cm / 3.1 in wide)

- Spare T-shirt and socks

- Phone charger
- A friend or coach to hand up attempt cards

## Terms

- **Bomb out**: To miss all three attempts on a lift; in a full-power meet that usually means no total.
- **Chief referee**: The referee who gives the commands on the platform; two side referees also judge each lift.
- **Classic (raw)**: A division with a non-supportive singlet and no supportive suits, shirts or knee wraps; belt, knee sleeves and wrist wraps are allowed within limits.
- **Flight (group)**: A group of lifters who take their attempts together; larger sessions split into flights, with lower previous totals lifting first in IPF rules.
- **Lot number**: A number drawn at weigh-in; if two lifters choose the same weight, the lower lot number lifts first.
- **Opener (first attempt)**: The first of your three attempts on a lift; chosen to be easy enough that you lift it with room to spare.
- **Rack height sheet**: The form where you record squat and bench rack and foot-block settings at weigh-in, so loaders set them before your attempt.
- **Spotter/loaders**: Helpers who load the bar and spot squat and bench; two to five are on the platform at a time in IPF rules.
