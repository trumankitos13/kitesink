01 — Consulting

Better systems behind the music.

Open Consulting

Workflow automation, operations improvements and practical AI for music teams. I help music businesses untangle repetitive work and awkward handoffs, and build practical tools around their data and process bottlenecks.

What keeps getting stuck?

Workflow automation

Connect the steps between your existing tools: repeated data entry, file handling, intake and task routing. Give the routine work a reliable path, with exceptions brought back to a person.

Possible projectTurn release asset intake into an organized checklist that flags missing files and routes the next task to the right person.

Every fitness dot. One instrument. Zero black boxes.

The periodization OS. You define the plan, macrocycle to set, and the engine checks it against your volume landmarks. Free from the start: the core app is in progress, with paid tiers to follow.
