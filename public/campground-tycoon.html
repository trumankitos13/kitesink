THE LOOP

Dirt today. Cabins by fall.

STEP 01
Plant your flag

Pick from 15 regions across a folded park map. Each one rolls a fresh, procedurally-generated plot of water, forest, prairie & hills.

STEP 02
Build the grounds

Clear plots and pitch tents, RV pads and cabins. Plant flower beds, set lamp posts, and watch your beauty score climb.

STEP 03
Keep guests happy

Check campers in, clean up, fix what breaks, shoo the bears. Anglers want water; hunters want quiet; sightseers want it pretty.

STEP 04
Survive the seasons

Ride the fall goldrush, then bank cash for the winter dead-zone. Weather, reviews and demand all turn with the calendar.

FOUR KINDS OF CAMPER

Everybody wants something different

Sightseers
PEAK: SUMMER → FALL

Want the grounds beautiful. Tip big when it's pretty and swoon over the elk — neglect your beauty score and they'll let you hear about it.

Anglers
PEAK: SPRING → SUMMER

Want a site near water and a short walk to the lakeshore. No lake on the rolled map? Expect sour reviews.

Hunters
PEAK: FALL

Want quiet wooded-edge sites and pay the most. Arrive in a huge fall wave, tolerate rough grounds — then vanish for the year.

Backpackers
PEAK: SUMMER

Low nightly rate, high volume, forgiving. Want trailhead sites and rent from the gear shack. Build it and they will come.
