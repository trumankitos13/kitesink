The loop

Dirt today. A park by fall.

  1. Plant your flag

    Survey the folded park map and settle one of 30 regions — each rolls its own water, forest, prairie, desert or bayou.

  2. Build the grounds

    Clear plots and pitch tents, RV pads and cabins. Plant flower beds and lamps, and watch your beauty score climb.

  3. Keep guests happy

    Sightseers, anglers, hunters and backpackers all want something different. Check them in, tidy up, mind the wildlife.

  4. Ride the seasons

    Spring runs to fall's goldrush, then the winter lull. Weather, demand and reviews all turn with the calendar.
