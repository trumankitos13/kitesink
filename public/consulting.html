I help music businesses untangle repetitive work and awkward handoffs, improve their operations, and build practical tools around their data and process bottlenecks.

For labels, artist teams, music companies and independent businesses whose tools haven’t quite caught up with the work.

Make the everyday work easier.

Start with the task that keeps getting stuck. The answer might be an automation, a simpler process or a tool that makes messy information useful.

Workflow automation

Connect the steps between your existing tools: repeated data entry, file handling, intake and task routing. Give the routine work a reliable path, with exceptions brought back to a person.

Possible projectTurn release asset intake into an organized checklist that flags missing files and routes the next task to the right person.

Operations improvements

Find where work slows down, clarify who owns the next step and simplify processes that have grown around the gaps. Make handoffs easier to follow and maintain.

Possible projectMap a campaign handoff across a music team, then build one shared view of responsibilities, status and outstanding decisions.

AI and data solutions

Make information easier to extract, classify, search and report on. Use AI where it helps, with human checks for uncertain results and decisions that need context.

Possible projectExtract key details from incoming documents into a review queue, or make an internal knowledge collection searchable with links back to its sources.

A focused project, built around your team.

  1. Understand the workflow

    Walk through the real steps, the tools you use and the places people have to work around them.

  2. Agree on the scope

    Choose a useful starting point. Define what the project includes and how we’ll check that it works.

  3. Build and test

    Try it against realistic cases, including missing information, failed steps and the exceptions people actually face.

  4. Document and hand over

    Leave clear instructions, ownership and a practical way for your team to use and maintain the result.

Know what you’re taking on.

We’ll make any recurring software costs, data access requirements and maintenance responsibilities clear before choosing a solution. Ongoing support, if needed, is something we agree on explicitly.
