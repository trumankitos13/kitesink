✱ How I work

Few things, done honestly

One thousand no's for every yes. I'd rather cut a feature than ship a vague one. Restraint is the whole craft.

Build in the open

Small experiments get a page; big builds get their own domain. The log is where I think out loud about all of it.

Data over vibes

From training volume to taste spectrums, I like measuring the right thing and then trusting it. Most products are downstream of one good metric.

Built with Claude

Every project here — the apps, the game, the writing, even this site — I designed and shipped with Claude as my pair. It's how one person covers the surface area of a small team without cutting corners on craft.

Keep a living knowledge base

Obsidian is the long memory. The tkl-orc vault ↗ keeps notes, decisions, and project context useful instead of merely archived.

Delegate, then check the work

Agents get a clear request, room to move, and a daily check-in. The leverage comes from good briefs and careful review, not from pretending the work runs itself.
