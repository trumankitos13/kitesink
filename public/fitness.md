## How the entries are graded

- Well supported: Meta-analyses, position stands, guidelines or several consistent trials.
- Rulebook: A federation rule or an official guideline value.
- Some support: A few trials or consistent observational data.
- Thin evidence: One or two small studies, or mixed findings.
- Coaching practice: Common coaching convention. No direct study.
