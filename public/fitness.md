# KiteSink Fitness

Free training and nutrition reference by Truman Kitos-Lindsey. The field guide holds 984 entries in 12 areas, and each entry names its sources and says how well supported it is. Around it: common claims checked against the research, a glossary, an index of key numbers and reference tables, and six calculators that run in the browser. Vantix, a periodization app, is the flagship project on the page.

## How sources are handled

Every entry names the sources it rests on, and each source was checked against its record. Papers link to their DOI; rulebooks, guidelines and articles link to their address. Articles are credited where an author's analysis or position shaped an entry. Every entry is written in this guide's own words. When a published entry changes what it says, the change is logged with its date at https://kitesink.com/fitness/sources.md.

## How the entries are graded

- Well supported: Meta-analyses, position stands, guidelines or several consistent trials.
- Rulebook: A federation rule or an official guideline value.
- Some support: A few trials or consistent observational data.
- Thin evidence: One or two small studies, or mixed findings.
- Coaching practice: Common coaching convention. No direct study.
