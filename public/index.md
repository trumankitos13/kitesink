# KiteSink

KiteSink is the one-person workshop of Truman Kitos-Lindsey. He makes tools for work, training and music, and has run the workshop since 2026. Everything on the site sits in five sections.

## The five sections

- [Consulting](https://kitesink.com/consulting.md): workflow automation, operations improvements and practical AI for music teams, and an AI concierge service for any industry.
- [Fitness](https://kitesink.com/fitness.md): Vantix (a periodization app), five free calculators, and a sourced field guide to hypertrophy, powerlifting, meet day, nutrition, diet and health.
- [Music](https://kitesink.com/music.md): Song of the Day (a daily music app), Stage First (the business side of a live show; its staffing app has been called Backline) and the Album Listening Club.
- Library (https://kitesink.com/library): every page on the site in one searchable index, including Campground Tycoon, a game whose demo plays in the browser.
- [About](https://kitesink.com/about.md): who is behind KiteSink and how to reach him.

- **A free 30-minute workflow teardown.** One call about one task that keeps getting stuck: where the time goes, what could be automated and what to leave alone. A limited number each month, first come, first served.
- **A one-page written plan.** No call needed. Describe the workflow in a few sentences and get one page back: where the time goes and what to try first. Free.
- **Early access.** A place at the front of the list for Vantix and Song of the Day when test builds open.

Ask through https://kitesink.com/consulting?via=ai-assistant#contact or email hello@kitesink.com.
