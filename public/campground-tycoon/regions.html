Eight true landscapes

Not a palette swap — a different country

Each family gets its own signature terrain system, flora and water character — Montana feels nothing like the bayou.

Pacific & Redwood Coast

Giant conifers, sea stacks and drizzle bands along the coast.

  • Cascades

    Washington · Moderate

    Long wet shoulder seasons and a steady stream of backpackers off the high trails.

  • Redwood Coast

    California · Moderate

    The tallest trees on Earth. Fog drips off the canopy; campers talk in whispers.

  • Crater Lake

    Oregon · Tough

    A rim of road around impossible blue water; snowbound for half the year.

Northern Rockies & Big Sky

Rocky ridgelines, alpine meadows and a braided river.

  • Big Sky

    Montana · Brutal

    Remote, short season, then an enormous fall hunting wave. Not for beginners.

  • Yellowstone Gate

    Wyoming · Tough

    A summer park crush of sightseers, then a hard mountain quiet.

  • Rockies

    Colorado · Tough

    Summer-loaded hiking and tourism; winter buries the whole place.

  • Sawtooths

    Idaho · Brutal

    Granite spires over cold alpine water. A short, glorious backcountry season.

High Desert & Canyon Country

Arroyos, mesas, slickrock canyons and gypsum dunes.

  • High Desert

    Oregon · Moderate

    Fly-fishing rivers in the canyons and big trail miles across the sage.

  • Red Rock

    Arizona · Moderate

    Inverted seasons — winter is peak, summer heat is the lull. Stunning vistas.

  • Canyonlands

    Utah · Tough

    Slickrock canyons and standing buttes; hikers pour in spring and fall, summer bakes.

  • White Sands

    New Mexico · Tough

    Gypsum dunes roll like snowdrifts. Golden-hour sightseers; a brutal summer sun.

Northwoods & Lake Country

Kettle lakes, granite shore and loons calling at dusk.

  • Boundary Waters

    Minnesota · Tough

    Remote paddle-in fishing and fall hunts; then brutal northern winters.

  • Northwoods

    Wisconsin · Tough

    Feast or famine: summer fish, fall hunt, dead snowed-in winters.

  • Great Lakes

    Michigan · Moderate

    Big-water walleye all summer; hunters in the hardwoods come fall.

  • North Woods

    Maine · Brutal

    A massive fall spike of hunters, then snowed in for months on end.

Appalachian Hollow

Ridge-and-valley hollows under a dense, fall-showpiece canopy.

  • Green Mountains

    Vermont · Moderate

    A leaf-peeper goldrush every fall; springs are muddy and slow.

  • Smokies

    Tennessee · Gentle

    Huge, forgiving volume — all-season sightseers and easy trail crowds.

  • New River Gorge

    West Virginia · Moderate

    Whitewater and climbers under the gorge rim; the fall color pulls big crowds.

  • Red River Gorge

    Kentucky · Gentle

    Sandstone arches over easy green hollows — a gentle place to learn the trade.

Plains & Hill Country

Tallgrass to the horizon, wildflower flats, striped badlands walls and big-sky sunsets.

  • Hill Country

    Texas · Moderate

    Spring festival tourism and fall deer leases over wide-open grassland.

  • Flint Hills

    Kansas · Moderate

    Tallgrass to the horizon. Upland birds burst from the bluestem come fall.

  • Badlands

    South Dakota · Tough

    Striped rock walls rise straight out of the grass. Summer crowds, hard winds after.

  • Sandhills

    Nebraska · Tough

    Grass-anchored dunes and spring-fed lakes; cranes darken the sky each spring.

Bayou & Blackwater

Braided marsh channels, cypress and gators in the shallows.

  • Bayou

    Louisiana · Moderate

    Spring redfish runs draw anglers; gentle winters keep a steady trickle.

  • Okefenokee

    Georgia · Moderate

    Blackwater swamp prairie — paddlers and anglers thread the cypress all year.

  • Delta Bottoms

    Mississippi · Moderate

    River-bottom hardwoods and sloughs — catfish all summer, duck blinds come fall.

Coastal & Tropical Shore

Dune bands, shore pines and palms, and a tide that keeps time.

  • Gulf Keys

    Florida · Gentle

    Year-round warmth with almost no off-season. The gentlest start of all.

  • Outer Banks

    North Carolina · Tough

    Surf-casting off wind-bent dunes. Hurricane season keeps a keeper honest.

  • Assateague

    Virginia · Moderate

    Wild ponies graze the dune line; beach campers pack the summer.

  • Sea Islands

    South Carolina · Gentle

    Warm tidal creeks behind the palms — gentle water, gentle winters.
