Campground Tycoon · The trail ahead · Dev log

Where we've been. Where we're headed.

54 updates and counting, grouped into eras. The path behind us is laid; the marker shows where we're camped right now; the dotted way ahead is still being cut.

  • Shipped
  • You are here
  • Still being cut

  1. Era 1 · Shipped The Foundation

    The core sim

    Live from day one

    • Core build loop — clear plots; pitch tents, RV pads and cabins; plant decor. Beauty, rating, guests and cash all live.
    • Four turning seasons — spring through winter scale demand and repaint the whole park — fall is the goldrush, winter the lull.
    • CAMP·OS booking terminal — the chunky CRT — reviews, pricing, shop, staff and the bank, all in amber-and-scanline.
    • Ranger's Almanac — a live in-game clock and calendar — time of day, the date and the season at a glance.
    • Weather & the Guest Book — severe events, guest reviews, and the first starter regions to settle.

  2. Era 2 · Shipped The Long Game

    Levels 8–20 stop being empty

    A late-game worth playing for

    • Facility upkeep — amenities wear with use; a worn facility earns half until serviced — plus the new Maintenance Ranger.
    • The Amphitheater & premium decor — ranger programs get a real venue, and the shop grows a higher tier of beauty pieces.
    • National Stamp Book — every stamp now pays a cash bonus, with tiered late-game stamps extending the collection.
    • Powered Tools & Junior Ranger Day — levels 16 and 11 unlock a productivity boost and a marquee family event.
    • A full ladder to Lv 20 — every level from 8 to 20 unlocks something real — no more grinding an empty bar.

  3. Era 3 · Shipped The Region Arc

    Every region becomes its own country

    The roster doubles, and terrain gets real

    • A versioned world-generator — every save pins its own terrain engine version, so existing parks never change underfoot.
    • Eight true landscape families — Sonoran desert, bayou wetland, northern Rockies, Appalachian hollow, northwoods, Pacific coast, plains and coastal shore — each with its own signature terrain system, not just a palette swap.
    • Thirty regions — the survey map doubles from fifteen, every one carrying real terrain identity.
    • Field Guide — wild residents, rare moments and one-per-map landmarks — arches, hot springs, lighthouses — in every new region.
    • Landscaping set in earth — planted trees, dug ponds and regraded meadows finally survive save and load.

  4. Era 4 · Shipped The Park Service

    A career bigger than any one park

    The meta layer arrives

    • Region Commissions — a charge unique to every region — complete one and every park you run gets a permanent demand bump.
    • Park Legacies — enshrine a park in the National Registry at Lv 15 + 85 rating — a ceremony, not a wipe.
    • The Park Service screen — legacies, commissions, the cross-park stamp book and your service roster, all in one place.
    • Yellowstone, the flagship — geyser basins, wolf packs and bison jams — locked until three parks are enshrined.
    • Groundwork for launch — gentler storms, durable device saves and the first accessibility settings, aimed at the phone launch ahead.

  5. Era 5 · Shipped The Living Regions

    The world keeps breathing

    Even when you’re not clicking

    • Regional Rhythms — tidewater on the coast, snowpack in the Rockies, drought in the desert — each region’s land visibly rises and falls with the season.
    • Ambient life — birds drift across every region’s sky, and campfires glow at occupied sites after dark.
    • Signature Festivals & Outings — a once-a-year festival and a headline guided outing, unique to every region.
    • Familiar Faces — regulars build a real bond over time, Newcomer through Beloved, with named friends and their own stories.
    • Staff Careers — every hire ranks up and earns a specialization — Concierge, Naturalist Guide, Plowmaster and more.

  6. You are here

    Era 6 · In progress v1.5 · Mobile Launch

    What’s ahead

    The trail we’re cutting right now

    • Native iOS & Android — the whole sim, wrapped for the App Store and Google Play — same cozy pace, on the couch.
    • A free demo, first — play free, then unlock the Full Park Pass — no ads, no pay-to-win, ever.
    • Durable, cloud-ready saves — saves that survive a backgrounded app and travel with you across devices.
    • Accessibility & performance polish — text sizing, high contrast, colorblind-safe palettes and a low-end performance pass.
    • Then: the next Park Service arc — Ranger Ranks, Region Mastery Stars and a Legacy Hall of Fame are sketched out for after launch.
