The Daily

One song, every day.

Each pick arrives with the weight of a print feature: a dek to set the scene, two paragraphs on why it matters, the facts, and the lineage — the songs it descends from and the ones that wouldn't exist without it. Then short liner notes from the editor's file: a moment, a quote, a fact. You don't skim it. You read it.

The Almanac

A year of listening, drawn as a portrait.

Collect the songs that stay with you and your Library reframes them — not as a list, but as a data-portrait of your year. A taste spectrum sized by what you actually played, the decades you spanned, your tempo range, and a streak grid that rewards showing up. A year-in-review that's always in progress.
