Questions

Is it free?

Yes — completely. No ads, no subscription, no in-app purchases. One song a day, on the house.

Do I need an account?

No. The app works fully signed-out. Sign in with Apple or Google only if you want your collected songs to sync across devices — that’s the single gated feature.

Where does the music play?

Wherever you already listen. Each song links out to Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, or Bandcamp — your choice. We don’t host audio; we hand you the story and the door.

Who picks the songs?

Real human editors who know the records. A person reads, writes, and signs off on every pick before it goes out.

When does it update?

Once a day, timed for your morning. One gentle notification, then it leaves you alone.

What’s “The Almanac”?

Your library, reframed as a year-in-review data-portrait — taste spectrum, decades spanned, tempo range, listening streaks. It updates every time you collect a song.
