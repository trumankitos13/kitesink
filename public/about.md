# About Truman Kitos-Lindsey and KiteSink

Truman Kitos-Lindsey builds apps, games and tools as one person. KiteSink is the workshop where all of it lives: a daily music app, an isometric tycoon game, a fitness app for serious lifters, and a consulting practice.

## In his words

"I care about craft, restraint, and finishing things. I'd rather ship six honest projects than talk about forty."

He spent his twenties splitting time between a squat rack, a record shelf and a text editor, and stopped treating those as separate hobbies. KiteSink is the one place everything lives together.

## How he works

- Small experiments get a page. Big builds get their own home.
- He would rather cut a feature than ship a vague one.
- He likes measuring the right thing and then trusting it.
- Every project is designed and shipped with Claude as a pair, with clear briefs and careful review.
