Backline · for working musiciansWIP · prototype

Your drummer bailed. The show goes on.

Find a sub nearby who’s free tonight, watch a 30-second reel of them actually playing, message, book and pay — all before soundcheck.

  • 01Hit SOSPick the role and the night. Only players who marked themselves free get pinged.
  • 02Watch them playA 30-second reel of the real thing.
  • 03Send the offer, pay on holdRole, time, venue, fee. Money is held and released after the show.

Album Listening Club · in personMonthly

Three albums, one quiet afternoon, once a month.

A listening club for the music-curious — welcoming, not gatekept. No ticket, no RSVP, nobody quizzed at the door.

  • 01PromptsAn optional prompt every fifteen minutes or so: composition, influence, emotional tone, production.
  • 02Best PickA vote at the end of every session. The winner enters the Album Hall of Fame.
  • 03Nothing compulsoryEvery ritual is opt-in. Listening quietly counts as taking part.
