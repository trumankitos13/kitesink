Stage First · the staffing sidePrototype · staging beta

Your drummer bailed. The show goes on.

Find a sub nearby who’s free tonight, watch a reel of them actually playing, message, book and pay, all before soundcheck. This is the staffing side of Stage First, the app called Backline until now. Its other side, a closeout that turns the night’s ticket reports into one approved report, is at design stage.

  • 01Hit SOSPick the role and the night. Only players who marked themselves free get pinged.
  • 02Watch them playA reel of the real thing.
  • 03Send the offer, pay on holdRole, time, venue, fee. Money is held and released after the show. Payments are still in test mode.

Album Listening Club · in personBack for 2027

Three albums, one quiet afternoon, once a month.

A listening club for the music-curious: welcoming, not gatekept. No ticket, no RSVP, nobody quizzed at the door.

Starting fresh for 2027. The inaugural session is Saturday 30 January, at midday. After that, the last Saturday of every month.

  • 01PromptsAn optional prompt every fifteen minutes or so: composition, influence, emotional tone, production.
  • 02Best PickA vote at the end of every session. The winner enters the Album Hall of Fame.
  • 03Nothing compulsoryEvery ritual is opt-in. Listening quietly counts as taking part.
