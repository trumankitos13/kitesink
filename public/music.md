# Music at KiteSink

Three music projects by Truman Kitos-Lindsey, plus consulting for music teams.

## Song of the Day

One curated song every day, with the story, the lineage and the liner notes to go with it. An iOS app for deep listening. **Status: coming soon.** It is not on the App Store yet. The app works fully signed out; signing in with Apple or Google is only for syncing collected songs across devices. More: https://kitesink.com/song-of-the-day

## Backline

A booking network for working musicians. Find a sub nearby who is free tonight, watch a reel of them playing, then message, book and pay before soundcheck. **Status: prototype.** More: https://kitesink.com/backline

## Album Listening Club

An in-person club: three albums, one quiet afternoon, once a month. It starts fresh in 2027 with an inaugural session on **Saturday 16 January 2027 at midday**, then meets on the last Saturday of every month. Free, no ticket, no RSVP. Venues rotate and are posted in Discord. Details: [https://kitesink.com/listening-club.md](https://kitesink.com/listening-club.md)

## For music teams

Workflow automation, operations improvements and practical AI for labels, artist teams and music companies: [https://kitesink.com/consulting.md](https://kitesink.com/consulting.md)
