The idea

A room where people actually sit, listen, and talk.

We explore and discover music together — discovery balanced with nostalgia, classics and new releases side by side. Musicians, music nerds and any curious fan belong here equally.

The format

One afternoon, three albums, start to finish.

The last Saturday of every month, midday, around three hours — longer when the venue allows. The 2027 season opens with an inaugural session on Saturday 30 January. Locations rotate and are posted in Discord. That month’s three albums play as the soundtrack for the whole session, in a quiet, comfortable atmosphere built for conversation and shared appreciation.

Prompts

An optional prompt every fifteen minutes or so: composition, influence, emotional tone, production. Any song from any of the three albums. Take it up or keep listening.

Best Pick

A vote at the end of every session. The winner enters the Album Hall of Fame, and whoever submitted it picks a genre for next month.

Nothing compulsory.

Every ritual is opt-in. Plenty of people spend a first session saying nothing at all, and that counts as taking part.

Monthly rhythm

Last Saturday of the month, all year.

The 2027 season starts with the inaugural session on Saturday 30 January. After that the club meets on the last Saturday of every month, so you can plan around it. Everything else runs on the same four-week rhythm.

Session day The week after 3 weeks out 1 week out then round again

  1. 3 weeks out

    Genres announced — one chosen by last month’s Best Pick winner, two open.

  2. 1 week out

    Submissions close, the three albums are posted, and the venue and exact start time go up in Discord.

  3. Session day

    Doors at midday, three albums start to finish, prompts throughout, Best Pick voted before everyone leaves.

  4. The week after

    Playlist updated with one track per album, Hall of Fame entry added, next month’s genre pick opened.

Rules

The albums, and the few rules around them.

Three members each submit an album from a pool that lives in Discord, and the three play as the whole session’s soundtrack.

Short version: turn up, listen, be kind to whoever picked the record.

  • Turning up

    No ticket, no RSVP. Slip in and out between albums rather than mid-record, and bring a friend if you’d rather — no notice needed.

  • Submitting

    Submitters say a few words about why they chose their album, or have them read out. The rotation opens to you after three months of attendance; submissions close a week before.

  • Genres

    Genres rotate and none of last month’s three may repeat, so discovery and nostalgia keep trading places. The Best Pick winner names one genre; full albums only.

  • In the room

    Records play start to finish — no skipping, no requests. Talk freely, at a level the album can live with. Phones on silent.

  • Voting

    One vote each and never for your own pick. The winner takes a Hall of Fame place and next month’s genre.

  • Conduct

    Disagree with the record, never with the person who brought it. Snobbery and gatekeeping are the only things actually against the rules.

Side B

The year ends with the Best of the Year Listening Party.

Everyone votes their top three songs of the year. The top three become Best of the Year, and those members pick the off-season albums.

Questions & contact

Questions people ask before their first one.

I don’t really know anything about music.

Then you are exactly who this is for. Half the room can’t name a chord — “I liked the drums” is a completely respectable contribution.

Do I have to talk?

No. The prompts are optional and plenty of people spend a first session just listening. Nobody goes round the circle.

Can I come on my own?

Most people do, including the regulars on their first visit. Someone will say hello to you before the first record starts.

Do I have to submit an album?

Not for your first three months — and never if you’d rather not. The rotation is an option, not a duty.

What if I’m late, or can’t stay?

Slip in between albums and leave whenever you like. An hour of one record is a perfectly good session.

What does it cost, and where is it?

Nothing but your own drink. Venues rotate, and the address for the next one is pinned in Discord a week ahead.
