Album Listening Club

Three albums, one quiet afternoon, once a month. A listening club for the music-curious — welcoming, not gatekept.

Join the Discord → Last Saturday · midday · ~3 hrs
01 / IDEA

A room where people actually sit, listen, and talk. We explore and discover music together — discovery balanced with nostalgia, classics and new releases side by side. Musicians, music nerds, and any curious fan belong here equally.

02 / FORMAT

The last Saturday of every month, midday, around three hours — longer when the venue allows. Locations rotate, posted in Discord. That month's three albums play as the soundtrack for the whole session, in a quiet, comfortable atmosphere built for conversation and shared appreciation.

03 / ALBUMS

Three members each submit an album every month. Genres rotate — no repeating last month's genres — and the pool lives in Discord. After three months of attendance, the rotation opens to you. Submitters share why they chose their album, or have it read for them.

Full rules & deadlines in Discord →
04 / RITUALS

Prompts — optional discussion prompts every ~15 minutes: composition, influence, emotional tone, production. Any song from any of the three albums.

Best Pick — a monthly vote; the winner enters the Album Hall of Fame and picks a genre for next month.

The Playlist — one featured track from every album, growing over time.

05 / SIDE B

The year ends with the Best of the Year Listening Party. Everyone votes their top three songs of the year — the top three become Best of the Year, and those members pick the off-season albums.

Come sit with an album.
