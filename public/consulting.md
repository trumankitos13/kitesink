# KiteSink Consulting

Independent consulting by Truman Kitos-Lindsey. Two offers: workflow, operations and AI work for music teams, and an AI concierge for any industry.

## For music teams

Workflow automation, operations improvements and practical AI for labels, artist teams, music companies and independent businesses whose tools have not caught up with the work.

- **Workflow automation.** Connect the steps between the tools a team already uses: repeated data entry, file handling, intake and task routing. Routine work gets a reliable path, and exceptions come back to a person. Example: release asset intake becomes a checklist that flags missing files and routes the next task to the right person.
- **Operations improvements.** Find where work slows down, make clear who owns the next step, and simplify processes that have grown around the gaps. Example: map a campaign handoff across a team, then build one shared view of responsibilities, status and open decisions.
- **AI and data solutions.** Make information easier to extract, classify, search and report on, with human checks for uncertain results. Example: pull key details from incoming documents into a review queue, or make an internal knowledge collection searchable with links back to its sources.

### How a project runs

1. Understand the workflow: the real steps, the tools, and where people work around them.
2. Agree on the scope: a useful starting point, what is included, and how we will check it works.
3. Build and test against realistic cases, including missing information and failed steps.
4. Document and hand over, with clear instructions and ownership.

Recurring software costs, data access needs and maintenance responsibilities are made clear before a solution is chosen. Ongoing support is agreed explicitly if it is needed.

## The AI concierge

A one-time or ongoing role for a business in any industry.

1. **Learn.** Your industry, your tools, and the work as you do it today.
2. **Observe.** Truman sits in on the real work, in person or over Zoom, and sees where the time goes.
3. **Improve.** You choose together what to fix first, and he builds the fix.

The fix can take three forms:

- **AI automation:** a model does the reading, sorting or drafting, and a person checks what matters.
- **Deterministic programs:** where the rules are fixed, ordinary code does the job and gives the same answer every time.
- **Custom software:** built for the team and maintained by Truman. It costs more, and there is a recurring cost to keep it running and up to date.

One-time means learn, observe, fix what was agreed and hand it over. Ongoing means he stays on, keeps watching the work and keeps improving it.
