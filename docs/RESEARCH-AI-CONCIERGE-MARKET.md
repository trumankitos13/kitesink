## 2. How they show proof (highest priority)

Four formats, from weakest to strongest. All of them are self-reported.

1. **Founder role and years only.** Indie Dojo: "a veteran music executive with over 30 years of
   experience." No logos, testimonials, metrics or guarantees. *This is where /consulting is now,
   minus the years.* [4]
2. **Vendor-wide outcome average.** SignalARC: "average client recovers 5 to 10 hours per week",
   "most clients recover the fee within 5 weeks". No sample size or named clients, and the payback
   figure rests on a hypothetical. [3]
3. **Risk reversal.** Deferred setup fee (Perpetual Concierge), 90-day go-live guarantee and audit
   credit (Main & Machine), money-back guarantee (SignalARC, unconfirmed). [1][2][3]
4. **One named client, dated before/after metric, method disclosed.** Perpetual Concierge, NJ
   Antiques, 25 Feb–4 Mar 2026: 85 calls, 74 qualified leads, about USD 12.75 AI usage, about
   USD 0.17/lead vs USD 5–50 for other channels. It states what the figure excludes. [2]
   *Weakness a buyer will spot:* including the USD 300 retainer gives about USD 4/lead, and the
   74 leads came from 54 unique numbers. **Lesson: publish the fully loaded figure yourself.**

None of the verified pages leaned on **client logos, partner badges (OpenAI, Anthropic, Zapier,
Make, n8n) or testimonials** as primary proof. For fractional AI roles, one practitioner advises
buyers to ask for: one real implementation including what went wrong and how it was fixed, two
references they can call, AI work from before 2022, and a paid test audit (GBP 500–1,500). She also
notes no recognised certification exists. This is opinion, not research. [7]

## 3. Service-page practice: pricing transparency (high confidence)

NN/g usability studies (Nielsen 2006, Loranger 2013; qualitative, B2B) found [9][10]:
- Business buyers rank pricing as the information they most need, yet many sites leave it out.
- When prices were hidden, participants went to competitors. Sites with clear pricing made the
  shortlist more often.
- Hiding prices reads as evasive and suggests "if I have to ask, I can't afford it."
- **For custom work, show sample prices for typical engagements** rather than hiding pricing or
  building a calculator. Avoid over-precise or complicated pricing.

/consulting currently says *"Prices aren't published yet."* This is the clearest evidence-backed gap.

Not answered by this run: CTA and form design (booking link vs form, field count), and measured
conversion lift for any proof format.

## 4. Roadmap inputs for /consulting (inference, not tested)

1. **Proof line**: role and years, plus one named, dated before/after with its method stated
   (fully loaded). This needs facts from Truman.
2. **Sample prices**: 2–3 typical engagements with ranges, e.g. concierge setup, a workflow sprint,
   a monthly retainer. Benchmarks are in §1.
3. **One risk-reversal term** you can actually honour, e.g. fixed written quote, pay setup on
   go-live, or audit fee credited toward the build.
4. **Optional, later**: a short "what went wrong and how it was fixed" note, and a reference
   available on request.
5. **Positioning**: no competitor found combines music operations with AI and data work. Saying so
   plainly is a differentiator (small sample).
